## Built for forms with hundreds of fields

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Shows, doesn't just tell" icon="seti:pipeline">"Where is the tax ID?" opens the right tab and accordion, scrolls to the field and highlights it.</Card>
  <Card title="Fills with consent" icon="approve-check">Values go on a card: tick, edit, fill in with one tap, Undo if needed. The agent never writes to the form.</Card>
  <Card title="Answers from your documents" icon="open-book">Policies are compiled into rules once; a researcher handles the long tail and cites the section.</Card>
  <Card title="Reads the user's documents" icon="document">A passport scan or a payslip is read separately; only the values reach the chat.</Card>
  <Card title="Small context, large form" icon="rocket">A collapsed outline, the open section and what changed: about 1K tokens instead of 16K.</Card>
  <Card title="Any model, any client" icon="puzzle">Anthropic, OpenAI, Azure, Gemini, Bedrock, Ollama. Strict AG-UI: CopilotKit and any AG-UI client connect as they are.</Card>
</CardGrid>
