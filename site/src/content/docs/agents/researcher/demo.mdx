The researcher reads your business documents for the agent and answers in a few lines, with sources. The answers below are **recorded**: they are the real output of the benchmark runs, wrong ones included, replayed in the real chat. Tap a source to read the quoted passage.

<ResearchDemo />

## What to look for

- **An exact figure** ("the fee after 30 days"): the answer names it and the page it is on.
- **A question across documents**: the sources come from more than one document.
- **A question the documents do not answer**: the right answer is "that is not in the documents", not a guess.
- **Two clauses that disagree**: the answer should say so and cite both.

How often each of these goes right, on which model and at what cost: [Benchmarks](../../../get-started/benchmarks/).
