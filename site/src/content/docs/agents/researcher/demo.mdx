The researcher reads your business documents for the agents and answers in a few lines, with sources. Here it works on test documents written for this benchmark, about fictional companies.

## Ask it yourself

Pick a set of documents, then type a question or tap one of the starters. The answer comes with the documents and sections it used. **Short** is one search and one answer; **Detailed** searches and reads in several steps; **Auto** chooses by the question and offers "Look deeper" after a short answer. In a detailed answer, words the researcher writes before a search step are removed as soon as it takes that step; the answer then appears as it is written.

The Short / Detailed switch belongs to this demo. Inside the agents the researcher has one mode today.

## Recorded answers, with the benchmark's checks

These answers are **recorded**: the real output of the benchmark runs, wrong ones included, replayed in the real chat. Each one names the documents and sections it used, and says which of the benchmark's checks it passed. Switch the model to compare the same question on both.

<ResearchDemo />

## What to look for

- **A question across documents**: the sources come from more than one document.
- **A question the documents do not answer**: the right answer is "the documents do not say", not a guess.
- **Two passages that disagree**: the answer should say so and cite both.

How often each of these goes right, on which model and at what cost: [Benchmarks](../../../get-started/benchmarks/#the-researcher).

## How the answers are checked

For every question, QA wrote down in advance the points a right answer must contain: the figure or the term, and the document it is in. A script checks which of them the answer contains, whether every number in it is in the documents it cites, whether it names its sources, and how long it is. No AI grades another AI. The script checks facts, not style: [how it's scored](../../../get-started/benchmarks/#how-its-scored).

## The documents

Generator programs written by our QA produced them for this benchmark: a policy handbook, a product catalogue, a services contract and a technical manual. The companies, people and places in them do not exist, so the model cannot answer from memory, every right answer is known in advance, and no real company's data is used. They are checked by code and by QA, not by a subject expert. [Read every document in full](../documents/).
