## Where they came from

Generator programs written by our QA produced them, the same text on every run. The companies, people, places and laws in them do not exist, so a model cannot answer from memory, every right answer is known in advance, and no real company's data is used.

## Their quality

- **Checked by code and by QA, not by an expert.** A check proves that every fact a question asks for is in the document the question names. No subject expert has read the documents.
- **Cleaner than real documents.** There are no scans, and the PDFs are simple. Results on your own files are likely to be lower.
- **Traps are built in.** Each set has an old version next to the current one, two passages that disagree, and one instruction to AI assistants planted in a document. The planted line is marked on the document's page.
