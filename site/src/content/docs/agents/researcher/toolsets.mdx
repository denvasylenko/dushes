## Documents

The researcher gets a read-only view of the documents its scope allows and a ranked search over them. It reads the sections a question needs and cites them; the chat shows each source as a chip. How the documents get there: [Business documents](../../shared/business-documents/); how retrieval works: [How documents are retrieved](../../how-it-works/knowledge/).
