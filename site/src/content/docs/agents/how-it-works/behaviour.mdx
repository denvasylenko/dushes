Each example is one line from a person, what the agent does with it, and what changes that. "Fixed" means no setting changes it: it is how the package works.

## Do it, or ask one question

**"Book a table for Friday."** The agent fills in what it can and asks only what is missing, **one question at a time**. A choice comes as a card with its options to tap, not as a list in the text.

- **Setting:** fixed.

## Undo instead of "are you sure?"

**"Set the guests to 4."** The value is filled in at once, with **Undo** on the card. Nothing asks "are you sure?".

**"Send it."** Sending is the one step that asks first: a confirmation card names what is sent, and nothing goes out until the person taps it.

- **Which tools ask first:** `confirm_tools` in `formguide.yaml`, for example `[submit_application]`. A tool on that list always waits for the person's yes. [Configuration](../../../reference/configuration/).
- **How long Undo stays on a card:** the widget's `undoSeconds` (default 20). [The form plugin](../../../frontend/widget/#the-form-plugin).

## Answer what was asked

**"What does full cover cost?"** The answer is the price, plus at most one next step. Not an overview of the form, not a list of everything else.

- **Dushes' reply length:** `style` when you build `Dushes(...)`: it replaces the default "one to three short sentences". [Extend it for your app](../../site-agent/#extend-it-for-your-app).

## "How do I get to…" gets an answer

**"How do I get to the loan form?"** Dushes answers where it is and offers to open it. It does not move the person. A page opens only on a direct request ("open the loan form") or a yes to the offer just made.

**"Open it."** right after that offer opens the loan form.

- **Setting:** fixed: the person's own words decide whether a page opens, never the model.
- **On a page with a form:** `stay_on_forms` on `Dushes(...)`, default `True`: Dushes answers in place while the person fills the form in, unless they ask to go.

## "Back"

**"Back"**, **"the previous one"** goes to the page the person had open before this one, by the conversation's own trail.

- **Needs:** the [decision model](../../site-agent/decision-model/) switched on; without it, the line goes to Dushes like any other.

## A two-digit year

**"Born 1st July '85."** The date field gets 1985-07-01. A two-digit year is this century when that is not in the future, otherwise the last one.

- **Needs:** DRAFT, to confirm whether this is the decision model's or the form's own.

## A card waits, and the person says "yes, and…"

The assistant asked "Fill in these three values?" on a card. The person writes **"yes"**: that answers the card, as a tap would.

**"Yes, and show me the statistics."** is a request, not an answer: the card keeps waiting, and the line is answered as an ordinary message.

- **Setting:** fixed: one rule for every host, in text and by voice (`formguide.voice.card_answer`, [Python API](../../../reference/python-api/#voice-helpers)).

## Experimental features, behind a code

**A visitor without a code** gets the assistant as it is: no decision model, no voice mode, nothing sent to their providers.

**A person who entered their code** under **+ → Experimental** gets the features their code opens.

- **Settings:** `EXPERIMENT_CODES` and `VOICE_CODES` in the host's environment; a host that knows its own people decides per run with `Dushes.run(..., layer=True)`. [Experimental features](../../shared/experimental-features/).

## The limits

**A turn that keeps calling tools** stops after a set number of rounds and answers with what it has.

- **Setting:** `max_tool_rounds` in `formguide.yaml`, default 10.
- **Voice mode:** a conversation's length and money, and a daily budget: [Voice mode, every option](../../shared/voice/#every-option).
- DRAFT: the public demo's visitor limits, if they are a host setting.

- **Recommend, don't push.** The business decides what to recommend; the agent decides when it fits. Asked for "the cheapest plan", the agent picks the cheapest and names the business's recommendation once, with its reason; it never preselects it, never repeats it after a no. Asked "what do you recommend?", it recommends.
- **Thresholds by action.** How sure the decision model must be will depend on what it does: opening a page asks less certainty than replacing a value someone already filled in. Sending stays never.
- **"A or B?"** When two readings fit about equally ("set 18" with two fields it could be), one card with both, instead of a guess or an open question.
- **Preferences between visits.** A host setting to let the assistant remember a person's preferences from one visit to the next.
