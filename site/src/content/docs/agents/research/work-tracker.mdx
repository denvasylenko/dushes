<Aside type="caution" title="A working tracker">
The statuses are as of 19 November 2026. The R&D team updates the tracker in its own document every day; this page is a copy of that day and is not kept up to date.
</Aside>

Each idea of the document is one row: every list item and every table row of [the review](../intentions/), plus the conclusions of the R&D team's research. Ideas we deliberately do not take stay in the table as a row with the reason.

Legend:

- **Rule N** is from the section "Rules for Dushes' agents". **F-N** is a function from the function map. **A-N** is an item of the order by agents. **H** is the prior probability. **S-N** is an item of "What the R&D team would do differently" on [the 2024–2026 page](../intent-systems/).
- The statuses, in order:
  - not taken;
  - not planned;
  - proposed: the R&D team proposed it, the CPO has not decided yet;
  - queued: with the Tech Lead, with an item;
  - built: with a commit hash;
  - accepted by QA: with numbers;
  - in production.
- "Already there" means that on 19 November it is already in the prompts or the code of main, but QA has no guard scenario for it yet.

Updates: the R&D team, once a day, from the messages of the Tech Lead and QA that the CPO forwards. **Last update: 19 November.** On that day there is not a single hash: the R&D team saw no work started on these ideas.
