<Aside type="caution" title="Experimental: only with an activation code">
Voice mode works only after you enter your personal activation code: tap **+** in the message field, choose "Experimental voice mode" and enter the code you got from whoever runs the site. Without a code it is off, and nothing is sent to the voice provider. It is a trial and can mishear. Once it is on, your voice is sent to OpenAI; the site keeps the transcribed text only.
</Aside>

This is not dictation. [Dictation](../what-the-chat-can-do/#voice) turns speech into text in the message field, and you still send it. In voice mode you have a conversation: you speak, the assistant answers aloud, and the chat shows the same messages it would show if you had typed.

## What a person does

1. **Tap + and choose "Experimental voice mode".** The row says "Needs your code" the first time.
2. **Enter the access code** and tap **Activate Experimental Mode**. The code is asked again after twelve hours.
3. **Allow the microphone** when the browser asks, and talk. An orb above the message field shows what is happening: **Listening**, **Thinking**, **Speaking**.

Later, a small orb in the message field starts voice mode in one tap.

## What happens when you talk

- **Your words appear as your message** while you speak, and are sent when you stop.
- **The assistant answers aloud**, in one or two short sentences. The written answer in the chat is the same answer.
- **What you said is filled in at once.** If you say your name, it goes into the name field with no card to accept. Numbers count as you say them: "50k", "one and a half million", "half a million". A line under the orb says what was filled ("First name: Anna") with **Undo**.
- **A card appears only when you have to decide**: a consent tick, an action that cannot be undone (sending the form), a value you did not say yourself, replacing something you typed by hand, a value the form refused, or a real choice between options. The assistant asks the question aloud, and you can answer with a spoken yes or no. The same card is never asked twice.
- **Speaking over an answer stops it.** So does a press on the orb in the web chat.

## Silent mode: the microphone button

The microphone button turns the microphone off. The conversation with the voice ends, you type, and the assistant answers in text without speaking. The same button turns the voice back on. The close button leaves voice mode.

The microphone is never on without the moving orb on screen. Closing the chat, leaving the page, starting a new chat or a failed start all end the conversation with the voice.

## The limits

A conversation is short on purpose, because an open one costs money every second, silence included.

| What ends it | What the chat says |
| --- | --- |
| Five minutes, or the conversation's budget | "Voice mode stopped: this conversation reached its limit. You can keep typing." |
| Thirty seconds of silence (not while a card waits for your answer) | "Voice mode stopped after a silence. Tap the microphone to go on." |
| The site's budget for the day | "Voice mode stopped: today's limit is reached. It is back tomorrow." |
| Your own budget for the day | "Your voice limit for today is reached. It is back tomorrow." |
| A second conversation at the same time | "Another voice conversation is going on. End it there, or try again in a minute." |
| A lost connection | "Voice mode stopped: the connection was lost. Tap the microphone to go on." |

The figures are the defaults; the host can change them ([every option](../../agents/shared/voice/#every-option)). The chat itself always keeps working: you can type at any time.

## Turn it on

The host must have the voice routes first: [Voice mode on the server](../../agents/shared/voice/).

<Tabs syncKey="voice-client">
<TabItem label="Web widget">

```html
<agui-chat url="/agent/run" voice-mode="on"></agui-chat>
```

```html
<!-- the voice routes somewhere else than /voice on the agent's host -->
<agui-chat url="/agent/run" voice-mode="on" voice-url="https://api.example.com/voice"></agui-chat>
```

With `voice-mode` on, the chat loads one more file, `agui-chat.voice.js`, and adds the row to the + menu. Nothing else changes until a person enters a code, and no key of the voice provider ever reaches the browser. To change a word, use `i18n` with `voice` in front of the key, for example `i18n: { voiceMenu: 'Talk to us' }`.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Swift client">

A native app opens one WebSocket to the host, sends the microphone as audio frames and plays the frames it gets back. The frames, the events and the rules for filling without a card are in the client contract: [CONTRACT.md, "Voice mode (experimental), voice contract 1"](https://github.com/denvasylenko/dushes/blob/main/packages/agui-chat-swift/CONTRACT.md). The same rules, for any client: [Voice mode on the server](../../agents/shared/voice/#the-events).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

`voice-mode` is not the `voice` option: `voice` chooses how [dictation](../what-the-chat-can-do/#voice) turns speech into text.
