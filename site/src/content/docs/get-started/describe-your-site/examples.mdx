The agents' own prompts live inside the package and you do not edit them. What you write is **configuration**: the site map, with its instructions, page descriptions and keywords, and one domain pack per form, with its persona, flow hints, glossary, rules, `pii` and consents. These are the words that make an assistant sound like your business and know your terms. Below are the demo's complete files, the ones the [live demo](../../live-demo/) runs on: open them, copy them, and write your own the same way.

<Aside type="tip" title="How to use them">
Each file is the real one from the repository's demo (`backend/demo/`). Start from the one closest to your business, keep its structure, and replace every text with your own. Each part is explained on [Site agent](../site-agent/), [UI agent](../ui-agent/) and [Develop a domain](../domain/).
</Aside>

## The site map: Main Street

A portal of three businesses on one site. What to look at:

- **`instructions`** at the top: the brief for the site agent, here one rule about not mixing the businesses and one about where to send loan comparisons.
- **`description`** of each page: one line on what it is.
- **`keywords`**: the words that send a message to that page from anywhere on the site.
- **`text`** of every tab, accordion and section: the facts people ask about (rates, prices, hours), in the page's own words. The site agent answers only from these.
- **`form`** on the pages that hold a form.

## Domain packs

One JSON per form. What to look at in each:

- **`persona`**: who the assistant is and how it speaks, in one or two sentences.
- **`flow_hints`**: which choice unlocks which part of the form.
- **`glossary`**: the business's facts about a field ("Full cover is offered only for cars at most 10 years old"): what the agent tells a person when they ask about it.
- **`pii`**: the fields the model sees only as `<filled>`.
- **`starters`**: the first suggestions in an empty chat.

## What the demo packs leave out

The demo forms are small, so their packs use only some keys. Your own forms will often need two more:

```json title="domain_packs/your_form.json (the additions)"
{
  "rules": ["After sending, we reply by email within one working day; the request can be changed or cancelled until then."],
  "consents": {"agree_terms": {"required": true, "doc": {"title": "Terms", "text": "The terms you accept by ticking."}}}
}
```

- **`rules`**: the business's rules the form's props do not show, including what happens after sending.
- **`consents`**: every agreement box, applied only by the person's own tick.

Every key of a pack: [Domain packs](../../../agents/ui-agent/domain-packs/). Every key of the site map: [Site agent, every option](../../../agents/site-agent/#every-option).
