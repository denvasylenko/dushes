<Aside type="caution" title="Experimental">
Voice mode is off until the host has voice codes, and each person needs their own code, entered under **+** in the chat. Without one, nothing is sent to the voice provider.
</Aside>

## Who may use it: codes and the pass secret

**What breaks without it.** Without `VOICE_CODES` there is no voice mode at all. Without `VOICE_SECRET` the passes are signed with a key made at every start, so every restart logs everyone out and they enter their code again.

**How to set it.** One code per person, 8 characters or more, and a fixed secret, in the host's environment:

```bash
VOICE_CODES="anna=correct-horse-battery,ben=another-long-code"
VOICE_SECRET="a-long-random-string-kept-with-your-other-secrets"
```

A host with its own sign-in needs no codes: it names the person of each request with `person_of`. How: [A host that already knows its people](../../../agents/shared/voice/#a-host-that-already-knows-its-people).

## The limits: money and seconds

**What breaks without it.** An open conversation is billed by the second, silence included. The defaults keep a forgotten microphone from running up a bill; a host with more traffic needs its own figures.

**How to set it.**

```bash
VOICE_DAY_USD=5              # this host's voice budget for a day
VOICE_PERSON_DAY_USD=1       # one person's daily budget inside it (0: none)
VOICE_MAX_SECONDS=300        # one conversation ends here
VOICE_MAX_USD=0.75           # or here, the voice and the agent's turns together
VOICE_AT_ONCE=1              # conversations at the same time on this host
VOICE_SILENCE_SECONDS=30     # this long without speech ends a conversation
```

When a limit is reached the conversation ends and the chat says why; typing always keeps working. Every key: [Voice mode, every option](../../../agents/shared/voice/#every-option).

## Interrupting, and silent mode

**What it affects.** Speaking over the assistant stops its answer, as does a press on the orb. The microphone button turns the voice off: the person types, and the assistant answers in text. Nothing to set; tell your users it works this way.

## What is filled from speech, and what still asks

**What it affects.** A value the person said aloud is filled at once, with no card: the line under the orb names it ("First name: Anna") with Undo. Numbers count as people shorten them: "50k", "one and a half million". A card still appears for a consent, an action that cannot be undone, a value the person did not say, replacing a value they typed by hand, a value the form refused, and a real choice between options.

**How to write it.** The same labels, options and consents as for typing ([UI agent](../ui-agent/)): a field whose label people say aloud ("date of birth", not "dob") is filled from speech; an unmarked consent is the one thing that would not wait for a tick, so mark every one.

## What is said aloud

**What it affects.** The assistant answers aloud in one or two short sentences, the same answer as in the chat. When a card waits, it asks the card's question aloud, and a spoken yes or no answers it.

**How to write it.** Short labels and option names, because they are read out. A card's question comes from the same props and pack as in chat.

## Where the audio goes

**What it affects.** The microphone audio goes to the voice provider of `VOICE_MODEL` (`gpt-live-1` by default) for as long as a conversation is open, under that provider's own data policy; read it and say so in your privacy notice. The microphone is never on without the moving orb on screen. Dushes keeps no audio; with `log_dir` set, it keeps a text record of each conversation (what was heard, what the voice said, the end and its cost).

## Checklist

- [ ] `VOICE_CODES` hold one code per person (or `person_of` names the person), and `VOICE_SECRET` is fixed.
- [ ] The day, person, conversation and seconds limits fit your traffic and budget.
- [ ] Labels and options are what people say aloud; every consent is marked.
- [ ] Your privacy notice names the voice provider and links its data policy.
- [ ] If you keep `log_dir`, its folder is protected like the rest of your personal data.
