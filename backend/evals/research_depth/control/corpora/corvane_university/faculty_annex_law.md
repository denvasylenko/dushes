# Faculty Annex: Law

Rules of the Faculty of Law that add to the Academic Regulations 2026.

## Admission

The faculty admits 150 first-year students a year. The entrance examination is held on 7 June; the pass mark is 70 out of 100. Applications close on 2 April.

## Placements and laboratories

A placement of 8 weeks is required in year 3. Laboratory groups have at most 20 students. A missed laboratory session is made up within 7 days or the course is failed.

## Thesis

The thesis is supervised by a member of the faculty and defended before a board of 4. The defence lasts 45 minutes. The faculty's own word limit for a master's thesis is 27,000 words.
