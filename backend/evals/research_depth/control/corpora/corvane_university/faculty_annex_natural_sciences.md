# Faculty Annex: Natural Sciences

Rules of the Faculty of Natural Sciences that add to the Academic Regulations 2026.

## Admission

The faculty admits 300 first-year students a year. The entrance examination is held on 24 June; the pass mark is 65 out of 100. Applications close on 7 April.

## Placements and laboratories

A placement of 12 weeks is required in year 2. Laboratory groups have at most 12 students. A missed laboratory session is made up within 10 days or the course is failed.

## Thesis

The thesis is supervised by a member of the faculty and defended before a board of 5. The defence lasts 20 minutes. The faculty's own word limit for a master's thesis is 27,000 words.
