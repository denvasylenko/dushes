## Paying

Tuition may be paid in 3 instalments, due on 15 September, 15 January and 15 April. A payment that is late by more than 10 days carries a late fee of €50.

## Refunds

A student who withdraws within 14 days of the start of the year gets the tuition back in full. A withdrawal until 31 October gets half. After that nothing is refunded.

## Scholarships

A merit scholarship of €1,500 a year goes to students whose average mark in the previous year was at least 85. Applications close on 30 June.
