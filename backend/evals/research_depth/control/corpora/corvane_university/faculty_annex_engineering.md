# Faculty Annex: Engineering

Rules of the Faculty of Engineering that add to the Academic Regulations 2026.

## Admission

The faculty admits 140 first-year students a year. The entrance examination is held on 24 June; the pass mark is 60 out of 100. Applications close on 1 April.

## Placements and laboratories

A placement of 12 weeks is required in year 2. Laboratory groups have at most 16 students. A missed laboratory session is made up within 14 days or the course is failed.

## Thesis

The thesis is supervised by a member of the faculty and defended before a board of 4. The defence lasts 45 minutes. The faculty's own word limit for a master's thesis is 26,000 words.
