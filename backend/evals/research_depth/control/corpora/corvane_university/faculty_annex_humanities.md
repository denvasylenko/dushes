# Faculty Annex: Humanities

Rules of the Faculty of Humanities that add to the Academic Regulations 2026.

## Admission

The faculty admits 370 first-year students a year. The entrance examination is held on 14 June; the pass mark is 55 out of 100. Applications close on 14 April.

## Placements and laboratories

A placement of 12 weeks is required in year 2. Laboratory groups have at most 20 students. A missed laboratory session is made up within 7 days or the course is failed.

## Thesis

The thesis is supervised by a member of the faculty and defended before a board of 5. The defence lasts 45 minutes. The faculty's own word limit for a master's thesis is 27,000 words.
