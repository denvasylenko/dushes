# Student Housing Rules 2026

## Rents

A single room costs €410 a month and a place in a shared room €290, heating and internet included. A deposit of €500 is paid with the first rent and returned within 30 days of moving out, less any damage.

## Contracts

A housing contract runs for the academic year. It can be ended early with 6 weeks' notice to the end of a month.

## House rules

Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00. Guests may stay up to three nights in a month. Smoking is not allowed in any building.
