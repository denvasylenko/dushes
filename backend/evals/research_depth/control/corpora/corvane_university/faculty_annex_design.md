# Faculty Annex: Design

Rules of the Faculty of Design that add to the Academic Regulations 2026.

## Admission

The faculty admits 150 first-year students a year. The entrance examination is held on 26 June; the pass mark is 65 out of 100. Applications close on 19 April.

## Placements and laboratories

A placement of 6 weeks is required in year 2. Laboratory groups have at most 10 students. A missed laboratory session is made up within 7 days or the course is failed.

## Thesis

The thesis is supervised by a member of the faculty and defended before a board of 3. The defence lasts 45 minutes. The faculty's own word limit for a master's thesis is 21,000 words.
