# Library Rules

A student may have 15 items on loan at a time, each for 4 weeks, renewable twice unless someone has reserved the item. An overdue item costs €0.30 a day. The reading rooms are open from 08:00 to 23:00 on weekdays and from 10:00 to 20:00 at weekends; during examination weeks they stay open until 02:00.

Group study rooms are booked online, up to 3 hours a day for a group. Interlibrary loans take about 10 working days and are free for thesis work.
