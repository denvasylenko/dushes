# Faculty Annex: Education

Rules of the Faculty of Education that add to the Academic Regulations 2026.

## Admission

The faculty admits 230 first-year students a year. The entrance examination is held on 13 June; the pass mark is 70 out of 100. Applications close on 4 April.

## Placements and laboratories

A placement of 6 weeks is required in year 3. Laboratory groups have at most 20 students. A missed laboratory session is made up within 10 days or the course is failed.

## Thesis

The thesis is supervised by a member of the faculty and defended before a board of 5. The defence lasts 30 minutes. The faculty's own word limit for a master's thesis is 20,000 words.
