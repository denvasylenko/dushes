# Faculty Annex: Medicine

Rules of the Faculty of Medicine that add to the Academic Regulations 2026.

## Admission

The faculty admits 190 first-year students a year. The entrance examination is held on 12 June; the pass mark is 65 out of 100. Applications close on 21 April.

## Placements and laboratories

A placement of 12 weeks is required in year 3. Laboratory groups have at most 10 students. A missed laboratory session is made up within 10 days or the course is failed.

## Thesis

The thesis is supervised by a member of the faculty and defended before a board of 5. The defence lasts 30 minutes. The faculty's own word limit for a master's thesis is 18,000 words.
