# Salon policies

## Cancellations and lateness
- Free cancellation up to 2 hours before the visit. Later cancellations or no-shows cost 50% of the service price.
- If you are more than 15 minutes late, the barber may shorten the service or reschedule it.

## Kids
- Kids haircuts are for children aged 3 to 14. Children under 3 are not served.
- A parent must stay in the salon during a kids haircut.

## Barbers' schedule
- Maria works Tuesday to Saturday.
- Omar does not work on Mondays.
- Alex and Ivan work Monday to Saturday.

## Payment and discounts
- We accept cards, cash and Apple Pay / Google Pay.
- First visit: 10% off, applied at the salon.
- Gift cards are sold at the front desk and are valid for 12 months.
