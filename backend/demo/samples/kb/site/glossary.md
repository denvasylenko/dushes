# Glossary

## Co-borrower
A person who signs the loan together with the applicant and is equally responsible for repaying it. At Aster Bank a spouse without a prenuptial agreement must be a co-borrower on a mortgage.

## Collateral
Property the bank can take if the loan is not repaid: the car for a car loan, the home for a mortgage.

## Down payment
The part of the price the borrower pays with their own money. At least 20% for a mortgage; 15% for families with two or more children under 18.

## Debt-to-income ratio
The share of monthly income that goes to loan payments. Aster Bank lends when it stays under 50% with the new loan.
