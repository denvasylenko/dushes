# Main Street partners

Main Street works with a few outside companies. They are named on the loan and booking pages.

## Northwind Insurance
Northwind Insurance is Aster Bank's partner for life insurance on consumer loans. Taking its policy lowers the loan rate by 2 points. The policy costs 0.9% of the loan amount a year and can be cancelled within 14 days for a full refund. Aster Bank does not require it.

## Keystone Valuation
Keystone Valuation is the independent appraiser Aster Bank accepts for used cars and homes. A valuation costs $90 for a car and $250 for a home, takes two working days, and is paid by the borrower. Another appraiser is accepted only if they are on the bank's list.

## Ledgerline Credit Bureau
Ledgerline is the credit bureau Aster Bank checks every applicant with. The check needs your consent, does not lower your credit score, and its result is valid for 30 days.

## Barber & Co gift cards
Barber & Co gift cards are sold at the salon and on the Book a visit page. They are valid for 12 months and pay for any service.
