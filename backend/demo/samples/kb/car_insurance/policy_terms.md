# Harbor Insurance: car policy terms

## Cancelling the policy
- You can cancel within 14 days of the start date and get the whole premium back.
- After 14 days the unused part of the premium is refunded, minus a 30 € administration fee.

## What each cover level includes
- Third-party liability: damage and injury you cause to other people and their property. No deductible.
- Standard: liability, plus theft of the car and fire.
- Full: standard, plus damage to your own car (also when it is your fault), glass, storms, floods and hail.
- Full cover is sold only for cars that are at most 10 years old.

## Not covered
- Wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, racing or track days, and driving without a valid licence.
- A driver who is not named on the policy, unless they are over 25 and drive the car occasionally.

## Price rules
- Drivers under 21 pay 40% more. Each at-fault accident in the last 3 years raises the premium (one: +25%, two: +60%).
- Three or more at-fault accidents: the application cannot be completed online.
- No-claims bonus: 5% off for every claim-free year at renewal, up to 30%.
- Windscreen repair has no deductible on standard and full cover.

## Claims
- Report a claim within 7 days: phone 8 800 700 80 90, Monday to Friday 9:00 to 18:00, or the 24-hour line for accidents.
- Keep photos of the damage and the other driver's details. A repair at a partner garage is paid directly.
