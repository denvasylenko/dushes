# Retail loan tariffs (2026 edition)

## Rates
- Consumer loan: from 21.9% per year.
- Mortgage: from 16.5% per year.
- Car loan: from 18.9% per year.
- Refinancing: from 19.5% per year.

## Rate discounts
- Salary clients of the bank: minus 1 percentage point. Applied at issuance; not shown in the application calculator.
- Life insurance on a consumer loan: minus 2 percentage points.
- Discounts add up, but the rate cannot go below 14.9% per year.

## Limits
- Consumer loan: from 30,000 to 5,000,000 ₽, for 12 to 60 months.
- Car loan: the car must be no older than 10 years at the end of the loan; a used car needs an independent valuation.
- Mortgage: down payment from 20%. Families with two or more children under 18: from 15% (family programme).

## Early repayment
- Partial and full early repayment are free of charge, on request in the app at least 1 business day in advance.
