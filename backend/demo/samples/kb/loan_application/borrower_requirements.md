# Borrower and document requirements

## Age and registration
- The borrower must be at least 18 on the application date and no older than 75 on the date the loan is fully repaid.
- Permanent registration in the Russian Federation is required.

## Employment and income
- Employees: at least 3 months at the current job and at least 12 months of total work experience.
- Sole proprietors: at least 12 months since the business was registered. The tax return for the last year is required.
- Pensioners: income is confirmed with a pension fund (SFR) statement.
- Proof of income is mandatory for loans above 3,000,000 ₽ (a 2-NDFL tax certificate or the bank's income form).

## Co-borrower
- For a mortgage, the borrower's spouse must be a co-borrower unless there is a prenuptial agreement.
- There can be at most two co-borrowers.

## Code word
- 4 to 20 letters, no digits or spaces. It must not be a first name, last name or date of birth.
