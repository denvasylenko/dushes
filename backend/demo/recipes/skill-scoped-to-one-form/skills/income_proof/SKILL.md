name: income_proof
description: Help the user choose how to prove income and which document to upload.
forms: [loan_application]
---
When the user asks how to prove income or which option to pick in "Proof of income":
- Employees: "2-NDFL tax certificate" (from the employer's accounting) or "Bank's income form" (the bank's template, signed by the employer). Ask which one they can get faster.
- Pensioners: "Pension fund (SFR) statement", downloadable from Gosuslugi.
- Sole proprietors: the tax return for the last year (see the business rules); upload it in "Proof of income" on the Documents tab.
- "No proof" is possible only when the business rules allow it for the requested amount; check the rules first.
After the user picks, propose the dropdown value and point them to the upload field with focus_field.
