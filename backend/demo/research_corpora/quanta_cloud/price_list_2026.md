# Quanta Cloud Price List 2026

Effective 1 February 2026. Prices in euro per user per month, excluding VAT.

## Plans

| Plan | Price per user per month | Users | Storage | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | free | up to 3 | 5 GB | community forum |
| Team | €12 | 3 or more | 100 GB pooled | email |
| Business | €29 | 5 or more | 1 TB pooled + 20 GB per user | email, chat, phone |
| Enterprise | on quotation (from €45) | 50 or more | unlimited | dedicated manager |

## Billing

Monthly billing is charged in advance on the same day each month. Annual billing is charged in advance for twelve months and gets a 15% discount on the monthly price.

Users added during a billing period are charged pro rata for the rest of the period. Users removed are credited only on annual plans, at the next renewal.

## Storage overage

Storage used above the plan's allowance costs €0.08 per GB per month, measured as the highest daily use in the month.

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price |
|---|---|
| Extra API calls (1 million) | €49 |
| Advanced audit log | €4 per user per month |
| Single sign-on (Team plan) | €2 per user per month; included in Business and Enterprise |
| Sandbox workspace | €99 per month |

## Discounts

Registered non-profit organisations get 30% off the Team and Business plans. Education discounts are available on request. Discounts cannot be combined, except the annual billing discount.
