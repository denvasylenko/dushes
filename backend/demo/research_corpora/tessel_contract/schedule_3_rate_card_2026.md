# Schedule 3: Rate Card 2026

In force from 1 January 2026, after the indexation of January 2026 (2.7%). Amendment No. 2 lowers the monthly fixed fees below by 1.5% from 1 April 2026; the hourly rates are not affected.

## Adding and removing Sites

A new Site is priced with the rates per square metre of the same type of Site. A Site removed with less than 3 months' notice is charged for the rest of the 3 months.
