# Schedule 1: Service Specification

## 1 Cleaning

Cleaning is done outside opening hours where the Site is not open 24 hours. The frequency for each Site is in Schedule 4; the standard tasks are:

| Area | Standard frequency | Task |
|---|---|---|
| sales floor | weekly | glass inside and outside |
| entrance and windows | monthly | deep clean |
| checkout area | monthly | glass inside and outside |
| customer toilets | daily | empty bins and wipe |
| staff rooms | monthly | empty bins and wipe |
| offices | twice daily | sweep and wet mop |
| stock room | every 3 months | disinfect touch points |
| loading bay | weekly | machine scrub |
| car park | daily | disinfect touch points |
| refrigerated area | daily | disinfect touch points |
| escalators and lifts | daily | machine scrub |
| roof and gutters | twice daily | sweep and wet mop |

Cleaning quality is checked by joint inspection of 10 areas at each Site every month; each area is scored 0 to 100 against the photo standard. The Site's score is the average.

## 2 Security

Guarding hours for each Site are in Schedule 4. Guards hold the Verdania guarding licence, patrol at least once an hour and log each patrol electronically. Alarm response outside guarding hours is within 30 minutes of the alarm.

## 3 Technical maintenance

Planned maintenance follows this plan for each asset in the asset register:

| Asset | Planned maintenance | What is done | Statutory |
|---|---|---|---|
| air handling unit | monthly | visual check and function test | no |
| chiller | every 6 months | full service to the maker's schedule | no |
| boiler | every 3 months | full service to the maker's schedule | no |
| fire alarm panel | yearly | statutory inspection by a certified person | no |
| sprinkler pump | monthly | statutory inspection by a certified person | no |
| emergency lighting | every 6 months | load test | yes |
| automatic door | monthly | statutory inspection by a certified person | no |
| roller shutter | monthly | visual check and function test | no |
| lift | monthly | full service to the maker's schedule | no |
| escalator | yearly | filter change and clean | no |
| refrigeration rack | every 3 months | load test | no |
| standby generator | every 3 months | statutory inspection by a certified person | no |
| distribution board | monthly | load test | no |
| CCTV system | monthly | filter change and clean | no |
| intruder alarm | every 3 months | statutory inspection by a certified person | no |
| dock leveller | every 6 months | load test | no |
| water heater | every 3 months | full service to the maker's schedule | no |
| extract fan | every 3 months | load test | no |

Reactive work is requested through the helpdesk and prioritised:

- Priority 1 (danger to people, a Site cannot trade, refrigeration failure): on site within 2 hours, made safe or working within 4 hours.
- Priority 2 (a service is degraded): on site within 4 hours, fixed within 24 hours.
- Priority 3 (everything else): on site within 24 hours, fixed within 5 working days.

Repairs of up to €350 in parts for one job are included in the fixed fee. Above that the Supplier quotes first, except for Priority 1 jobs, which are made safe at once.

## 4 Helpdesk

The helpdesk answers by phone and through the portal 24 hours a day. A call is answered within 30 seconds and every request gets a number and a priority at once.

## 5 Provided by the Client

Water, electricity, a lockable store room and a staff room at each Site, and access to the building management system.

## 6 Consumables

Cleaning materials are included. Washroom consumables (paper, soap) are charged at cost plus 8%.
