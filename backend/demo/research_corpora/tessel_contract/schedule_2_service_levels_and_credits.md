# Schedule 2: Service Levels and Credits

Under clause 1.2 of the Agreement this Schedule prevails over the other Schedules on service levels.

## 1 Response and fix times

| Priority | On site within | Fixed or made safe within |
|---|---|---|
| Priority 1 | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| Priority 2 | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| Priority 3 | 24 hours | 5 working days |

## 2 Monthly service levels

| Service level | How it is measured | Target | Critical |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cleaning quality | average inspection score of all Sites | 92.0 | no |
| Priority 1 jobs in time | share of Priority 1 jobs that met both times | 95.0% | yes |
| Planned maintenance done | share of planned jobs completed in the month they were due | 98.0% | yes |
| Helpdesk | share of calls answered within 30 seconds | 90.0% | no |

## 3 Service credits

For each service level missed in a month, the credit is 1% of that month's total fixed fees for each full percentage point (or each full point of score) below the target. In addition, each Priority 1 job that missed a time carries a credit of €500.

All credits of one month together are limited to 10% of that month's total fixed fees.

No credit is due where the miss was caused by the Client, by a failure of the Client's utilities, or by an event outside the Supplier's control that it could not reasonably have prevented.

## 4 Earning back

A credit for a service level that is not critical is cancelled if the Supplier meets that service level in each of the following 3 months.

## 5 Reporting

The Supplier sends the monthly performance report by the 8th working day of the following month. A late report is treated as a miss of the helpdesk service level by one point.
