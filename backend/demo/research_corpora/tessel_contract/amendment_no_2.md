# Amendment No. 2 to the Framework Agreement

Signed on 16 March 2026, in force from 1 April 2026. Under clause 1.2 this Amendment prevails over the Agreement and over Amendment No. 1 where they differ.

1. Clause 5.2 (payment): the Client pays each undisputed invoice within 45 days of receiving it. Invoices received before 1 April 2026 keep the term of 30 days.

2. Clause 5.4 (indexation): the yearly adjustment is limited to 4.5% instead of 3.0%, from the adjustment of January 2027.

3. Clause 8.2 (liability): the limit is 150% of the charges paid or payable in the contract year. The minimum of €2,000,000 stays.

4. In return for points 1 to 3 the Supplier lowers every monthly fixed fee in Schedule 3 by 1.5% from 1 April 2026.

5. All other terms stay unchanged.
