# Underwriting Guidelines - Personal Lines

Internal document, version 12, July 2026. Not for distribution to customers.

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines tell underwriters and branch staff which risks we accept, which need a referral and which we decline. When in doubt, refer.

## 2. Acceptance rules

1. Do not accept a home with a thatched roof without a referral to the senior underwriter.
2. A home built before 1900 needs a survey report if the buildings sum insured is above €800,000.
3. Homes used for business with visiting clients need the business-use endorsement.
4. Properties let to students or as holiday lets are not accepted on HomeGuard.
5. Cars worth more than €150,000 are referred to the high-value motor team.
6. Drivers aged 85 or over are not accepted as new customers; existing customers renew with a medical declaration.
7. Cars imported privately from outside Europe are not accepted.
8. Modifications that increase engine power by more than 10% must be declared and are referred.
9. Drivers with more than 6 penalty points in the last 3 years are referred.
10. A home left unoccupied for more than 60 days needs the unoccupancy endorsement before cover can continue in full.
11. Customers with a previous insurance refused or cancelled for non-disclosure are referred.
12. Pets of the breeds listed in the restricted breeds annex are not accepted.

## 5. Referral process

Referrals go through the internal service portal with the full proposal and any supporting documents. A referral is answered within 2 business days.
