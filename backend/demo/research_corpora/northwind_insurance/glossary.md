# Northwind Insurance Glossary

Plain-language explanations of the terms used in our documents.

## Actual cash value

The replacement cost of an item less an amount for wear and tear. The exact rules are in the policy wording of each product.

## Assignment

The transfer of the rights under a policy to another person, which needs our consent. Ask your branch if you are unsure how this applies to you.

## Beneficiary

The person who receives a benefit under a policy. Ask your branch if you are unsure how this applies to you.

## Broker

An intermediary who arranges insurance for customers and is paid commission. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Claim

A request for payment or service under the policy. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Co-payment

A share of each claim that the policyholder pays in addition to the excess. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Endorsement

A written change to the policy that adds, removes or changes cover. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Excess

The first part of each claim that the policyholder pays. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Exclusion

Something the policy does not cover. See the product's policy wording for the amounts that apply.

## Indemnity

Putting the policyholder back in the financial position they were in before the loss. Ask your branch if you are unsure how this applies to you.

## Insurable interest

A financial interest in the thing insured, needed for a valid policy. Ask your branch if you are unsure how this applies to you.

## Lapse

The end of a policy because the premium was not paid. See the product's policy wording for the amounts that apply.

## Loss adjuster

An independent expert who investigates and values a claim. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Market value

The cost of buying a car of the same make, model, age, mileage and condition just before the loss. Ask your branch if you are unsure how this applies to you.

## Material fact

Information that would influence an insurer's decision to accept a risk or set its price. Ask your branch if you are unsure how this applies to you.

## New for old

Settlement at the cost of a new item, with no deduction for wear and tear. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## No-claims discount

A reduction in the premium for each year without a claim. The exact rules are in the policy wording of each product.

## Period of insurance

The time during which the policy is in force. The exact rules are in the policy wording of each product.

## Premium

The amount paid for the insurance. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Pro rata

In proportion to the time or amount concerned. See the product's policy wording for the amounts that apply.

## Proposal

The information the customer gives when applying for insurance. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Renewal

The continuation of a policy for a further period. See the product's policy wording for the amounts that apply.

## Schedule

The document that shows the details of the cover a customer has bought. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Subrogation

Our right to recover what we paid from the person responsible for the loss. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Sum insured

The most we pay under a section of the policy. Ask your branch if you are unsure how this applies to you.

## Total loss

A car or item that cannot be repaired economically. See the product's policy wording for the amounts that apply.

## Underinsurance

A sum insured lower than the full value, which can reduce a claim payment proportionally. Ask your branch if you are unsure how this applies to you.

## Underwriter

The person who decides whether to accept a risk and on what terms. This term is used in all Northwind personal lines products.

## Unoccupied

Not lived in for more than the number of days stated in the policy. See the product's policy wording for the amounts that apply.

## Write-off

Another word for total loss. Ask your branch if you are unsure how this applies to you.
