# Northwind Motor - Tariff 2026

Effective for motor policies that start or renew on or after 1 March 2026. It replaces the Tariff 2025. A policy that started before 1 March 2026 keeps the Tariff 2025 until its next renewal. All premiums are annual, in euro, including insurance premium tax.

## How to calculate a premium

1. Find the base premium for the vehicle group and the region of the address where the car is kept overnight (Table 1).
2. Apply the no-claims discount (Table 2).
3. Add the young driver surcharge if any named driver is under 25 on the start date.
4. Apply the voluntary excess discount (Table 3) and the telematics discount if a Northwind telematics box is fitted.
5. Add optional extras (Table 4).

Discounts are applied one after another to the running total, in the order above, and the result is rounded to whole euro.

One claim in a year reduces the claim-free years by three, two or more claims reduce them to zero. A protected no-claims discount costs 9% of the premium after discounts.

## Young drivers

A surcharge of 38% of the base premium applies when any named driver is under 25 years of age on the start date or renewal date of the policy.

## Table 3 - Excess

The compulsory excess is €250 for every claim for damage to your own car. Drivers under 25 pay an additional €300.

Windscreen and window glass claims: excess €75; no effect on the no-claims discount.

Voluntary excess, added to the compulsory excess:

| Voluntary excess | Discount |
|---|---|
| €0 | 0% |
| €100 | 4% |
| €250 | 8% |
| €500 | 12% |
| €1,000 | 15% |

## Telematics

Customers who fit a Northwind telematics box and keep it active receive a 12% discount.
