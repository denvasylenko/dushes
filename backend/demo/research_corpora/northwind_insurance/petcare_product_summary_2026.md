# Northwind PetCare - Product Summary 2026

## Who can be insured

Dogs and cats living in Verdania, at least 8 weeks old. Dogs can be insured for the first time up to their 8th birthday, cats up to their 10th birthday. Once insured, cover continues for life as long as the policy is renewed.

## Premiums by breed tier

| Tier | Example breeds | Monthly premium (dog) | Monthly premium (cat) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | mixed breed, small terriers | €14.50 | €9.90 |
| 2 | spaniels, retrievers | €19.90 | €12.50 |
| 3 | shepherds, large hounds | €26.40 | €15.00 |
| 4 | giant breeds, brachycephalic breeds | €34.80 | €18.90 |

## Annual vet fee limits

| Plan | Vet fees per year | Third-party liability (dogs) |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | €2,000 | €1,000,000 |
| Plus | €5,000 | €2,000,000 |
| Lifetime | €8,000 | €2,000,000 |

## Excess and co-payment

A fixed excess of €90 applies per condition per policy year. From the pet's 8th birthday a co-payment of 20% of each claim applies in addition to the fixed excess.

## Waiting periods

Illness is covered 14 days after the start date; accidents are covered from the start date.

## Not covered

Pre-existing conditions, routine and preventive treatment such as vaccinations, neutering and dental cleaning, pregnancy, and grooming.
