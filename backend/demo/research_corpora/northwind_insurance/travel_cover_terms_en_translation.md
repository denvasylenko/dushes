# Northwind Travel Cover - Policy Terms (English translation)

Edition 2026. This is a translation of the German terms "Northwind Reiseschutz - Allgemeine Versicherungsbedingungen". In case of any discrepancy between this translation and the German version, the German version prevails.

## 1. Insured persons

The persons named in the policy schedule. The insured person may be at most 75 years old when the policy is taken out. Children under 18 are covered free of charge under the family tariff.

## 2. Medical expenses abroad

We pay medically necessary treatment abroad up to €2,000,000 per trip, including medically appropriate repatriation. The excess is €100 per claim. Dental treatment is covered only for pain relief, up to €400.

## 3. Cancellation

We pay the cancellation charges you owe under the travel contract, up to €5,000 per person, if you cannot start the trip because of an unexpected serious illness, an accident, the death of a close relative or the loss of your job. The insurance must be taken out at least 30 days before departure; otherwise a waiting period of 10 days applies.

## 4. Baggage

Baggage is insured up to €1,500 per person. For any single item we pay at most €350. Valuables are covered only while worn or kept in a locked safe.

## 5. Exclusions

Extreme sports (parachuting, diving deeper than 30 metres, mountaineering above 5,000 metres), travel against a travel warning issued by the government of Verdania, and pre-existing conditions treated in the last 12 months.

## 6. Making a claim

Call the 24-hour emergency line on +00 44 754 2824 before any hospital stay. Report claims within 31 days of your return.

## 7. Annual multi-trip option

The annual multi-trip option covers any number of trips in a year, each lasting up to 45 days. Winter sports are included for up to 17 days per year.
