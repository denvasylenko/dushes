# Operating Procedures: Machining

The standard procedures of the Machining function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-MAC-01 Tool change

Owner: Robin Kettridge, Machining. Last review: 9 March 2026. Next review after 12 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how tool change is done in Machining, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 90 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 3 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 48 hours, until Quality decides. A problem that is not solved within 2 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The safety officer confirms the setting within the same shift.
2. The operator measures the batch.
3. The specialist releases the previous step.
4. The head of department records the result within the same shift.
5. The team lead releases the setting.
6. The head of department signs off the label before the next step starts.
7. Quality releases the document with a second person.
8. The operator releases the previous step.
9. The operator checks the count with a second person.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 2 years in the ERP system. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| first-time-right | 85% | monthly |
| first-time-right | at most 2 per month | monthly |
| cost per case | 92% | weekly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one batch only.
