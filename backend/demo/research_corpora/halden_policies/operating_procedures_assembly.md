# Operating Procedures: Assembly

The standard procedures of the Assembly function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-ASS-01 Line changeover

Owner: Taylor Ironsby, Assembly. Last review: 10 April 2026. Next review after 36 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how line changeover is done in Assembly, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 60 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 5 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 72 hours, until the head of department decides. A problem that is not solved within 8 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The specialist confirms the count with a second person.
2. The safety officer confirms the result with a second person.
3. The operator records the label with a second person.
4. Quality records the result against the checklist.
5. The specialist confirms the previous step.
6. The shift leader confirms the result.
7. The operator signs off the result within the same shift.
8. The specialist checks the setting within the same shift.
9. The head of department records the document against the checklist.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 10 years in the maintenance system. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| repeat findings | 92% | quarterly |
| repeat findings | 97% | quarterly |
| on-time completion | 99% | weekly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one week only.
