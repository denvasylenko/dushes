## Rules

The owner checks once a year, in January, what has reached the end of its period and has it destroyed within 60 days. Destruction of paper is done by the contracted shredding service and recorded with a certificate. Personal data is never kept longer than the schedule because it "might be useful".

**General condition 1.** Customers with accessibility needs can ask for this document in large print, audio or another format at no extra cost.

**General condition 2.** We process personal data as described in our privacy notice. We keep records only for as long as we need them for the purposes stated there.

**General condition 3.** References to a law include that law as amended or re-enacted from time to time and any regulation made under it.

**General condition 4.** References to a law include that law as amended or re-enacted from time to time and any regulation made under it.

**General condition 5.** Copies of earlier versions of this document are kept in the archive and can be requested. They apply only to the period stated on their cover.

**General condition 6.** The parties will try to resolve any dispute by discussion between senior representatives before starting formal proceedings.
