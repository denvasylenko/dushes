# Company Car Policy

Version 5, in force from 1 January 2026.

## 1 Who gets a car

Grades G6 to G8, and field sales staff who drive more than 15,000 business kilometres a year.

| Entitlement | List price up to | Drive | Monthly charging or fuel budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| G6 | €38,000 | electric or plug-in hybrid | €250 |
| G7 | €52,000 | electric or plug-in hybrid | €350 |
| G8 | €68,000 | electric | €450 |
| Field sales (any grade) | €34,000 | electric | €250 |

## 2 Lease

Cars are leased for 48 months or 160,000 km, whichever comes first. An employee who leaves returns the car on the last working day. Ordering a car in the last 6 months before a known leaving date is not possible.

## 3 Private use

Private use is allowed, also by the partner living in the same household. Journeys abroad of more than 14 days need the fleet manager's consent. The taxable benefit is shown on the pay slip.

## 4 Damage and fines

Report damage to the fleet manager within 24 hours. The excess for damage that was the driver's fault is €300, and €600 from the third such damage within 24 months. Fines are paid by the driver.

## 5 Instead of a car

An entitled employee may take a mobility allowance instead: €520 a month for G6, €700 for G7 and €900 for G8.
