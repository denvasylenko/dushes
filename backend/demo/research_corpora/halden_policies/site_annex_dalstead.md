# Site Annex: Dalstead

Local rules for the Dalstead site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

290 employees. Site director: Quinn Kettridge. Safety officer: Robin Fenwright, +00 31 876 2122. Site emergency number: +00 11 964 3583. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 06:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00, 22:00-06:00. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 6 to 22 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

## Eating

The canteen serves the daily menu at €5.20 for employees; the company pays the rest. No meal vouchers are issued at this site.

## Getting here

330 parking spaces, of which 28 have a charger. The shuttle from Dalstead station leaves at 05:40, 13:40 and 21:35. Bicycles are kept in the shelter next to building B.
