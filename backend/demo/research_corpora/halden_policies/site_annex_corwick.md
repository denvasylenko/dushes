# Site Annex: Corwick

Local rules for the Corwick site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

540 employees. Site director: Riley Pellow. Safety officer: Jamie Osterby, +00 31 181 3788. Site emergency number: +00 11 677 8340. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 06:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 5 to 21 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

## Eating

The canteen serves the daily menu at €4.20 for employees; the company pays the rest. No meal vouchers are issued at this site.

## Getting here

450 parking spaces, of which 17 have a charger. The shuttle from Corwick station leaves at 05:45, 13:40 and 21:40. Bicycles are kept in the shelter next to building B.
