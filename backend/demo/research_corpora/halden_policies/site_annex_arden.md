# Site Annex: Arden

Local rules for the Arden site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

220 employees. Site director: Milan Halloway. Safety officer: Avery Tregarth, +00 31 743 5471. Site emergency number: +00 11 486 7254. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 06:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00, 22:00-06:00. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 3 to 17 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

## Eating

There is no canteen at this site; meal vouchers are issued as described in the Benefits Guide.
