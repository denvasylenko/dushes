# Site Annex: Marwick

Local rules for the Marwick site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

430 employees. Site director: Sam Ironsby. Safety officer: Sasha Tregarth, +00 31 692 3121. Site emergency number: +00 11 906 1356. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 06:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 3 to 16 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

## Eating

The canteen serves the daily menu at €4.80 for employees; the company pays the rest. No meal vouchers are issued at this site.

## Getting here

430 parking spaces, of which 7 have a charger. The shuttle from Marwick station leaves at 05:40, 13:35 and 21:40. Bicycles are kept in the shelter next to building C.
