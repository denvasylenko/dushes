# Site Annex: Linash

Local rules for the Linash site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

710 employees. Site director: Riley Jessop. Safety officer: Rowan Pellow, +00 31 171 2769. Site emergency number: +00 11 703 2928. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 06:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 3 to 21 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

## Eating

There is no canteen at this site; meal vouchers are issued as described in the Benefits Guide.

## Getting here

240 parking spaces, of which 12 have a charger. The shuttle from Linash station leaves at 05:40, 13:35 and 21:35. Bicycles are kept in the shelter next to building A.
