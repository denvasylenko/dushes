# Supplier Code of Conduct

Version 3, 2025. Every supplier signs this code before the first order (Procurement Policy, section 5).

## 1 Labour

No child labour and no forced labour. Working hours and wages meet at least the law of the country of work. Workers are free to join a union.

## 2 Safety and environment

A safe workplace, with accident figures reported to Halden Works on request. Suppliers with more than 50 employees hold a certified environmental management system, or have one within 24 months of the first order.

## 3 Integrity

No bribes, no facilitation payments. Gifts to Halden Works staff above €50 a year are not offered. A conflict of interest is disclosed.

## 4 Audits

Halden Works may audit a supplier with 10 working days' notice, and without notice where there is a serious suspicion. A serious breach that is not corrected within 90 days ends the business relationship.

## 5 Sub-suppliers

The supplier passes these rules on to its own suppliers and answers for them.

**Supplier condition 1.** Customers with accessibility needs can ask for this document in large print, audio or another format at no extra cost.

**Supplier condition 2.** Each party will act in good faith and will cooperate reasonably with the other to give effect to the purpose of this document.

**Supplier condition 3.** Customers with accessibility needs can ask for this document in large print, audio or another format at no extra cost.

**Supplier condition 4.** Copies of earlier versions of this document are kept in the archive and can be requested. They apply only to the period stated on their cover.

**Supplier condition 5.** This document and the documents it refers to are the whole agreement between the parties on its subject and replace any earlier understanding.

**Supplier condition 6.** If there is a conflict between a schedule and the main text, the schedule applies for the matter it covers and the main text applies for everything else.

**Supplier condition 7.** Any change to this document must be agreed in writing, except where it says that we may change a part of it by publishing a new version.

**Supplier condition 8.** If there is a conflict between a schedule and the main text, the schedule applies for the matter it covers and the main text applies for everything else.

**Supplier condition 9.** A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of that right, and a single or partial exercise does not prevent any further exercise of it.

**Supplier condition 10.** Each party will act in good faith and will cooperate reasonably with the other to give effect to the purpose of this document.
