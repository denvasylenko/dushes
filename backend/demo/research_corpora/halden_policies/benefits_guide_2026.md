# Benefits Guide 2026

A plain summary of the benefits Halden Works offers. The policies and the pension scheme rules are the binding texts.

## Pension

Halden Works pays 6% of gross salary into the company pension scheme. Employees pay at least 3%; each extra percent they pay, up to 2 more, is matched with half a percent by the company. Membership starts after the probation period.

## Health

A health check with the company doctor every 2 years, yearly from the age of 50. Flu vaccination each autumn at every site. Glasses for screen work are paid up to €150 every 3 years.

## Meals

A meal voucher of €9.50 for each working day at a site without a canteen. Sites with a canteen subsidise the daily menu instead (see the site annex).

## Getting to work

A public transport season ticket is subsidised at 50%. A bicycle can be leased through salary sacrifice for up to €4,000. Business journeys in a private car are reimbursed at €0.38 per kilometre.

## Family

Parental leave as in the Staff Handbook. A childcare subsidy of €120 a month for each child under 6. Two paid days a year to care for a sick child, on top of the legal entitlement.

## Service awards

| Years of service | Award |
|---|---|
| 10 | €500 |
| 20 | €1,500 |
| 25 | €2,500 and 2 extra days of leave in that year |
| 40 | €5,000 |

## Learning

5 training days and €1,200 a year, as in the Staff Handbook. Language courses are paid in full where the language is needed for the job.
