# Health and Safety Manual

Version 12, in force from 1 October 2025. It applies at every site; the site annex names the local safety officer, the assembly points and the first-aid rooms.

## 1 Basics

Every accident and every near miss is entered in the safety log before the end of the shift. An accident with more than 3 days of absence is reported by the safety officer to the accident insurer within 3 working days. New staff get the safety induction on their first day, before they enter a production area. Anyone may stop work they believe is unsafe, without asking first.

## 2 Forklift traffic

### 2.1 The rule

Work that involves forklift traffic is done only by people who hold the internal licence for it. The licence is renewed every 24 months by a refresher of at least 4 hours. No permit is needed, but the risk assessment of the area must be read and signed once a year. At least 1 trained person is present for the whole time.

### 2.2 Protection

Wear safety shoes, goggles, a face shield. Keep a distance of at least 1.5 metres between the work and people who are not part of it; mark the area where that distance cannot be kept.

### 2.3 If something goes wrong

Stop, make the area safe if that is possible without risk, call the site emergency number and then the shift leader. The equipment involved is not used again until the safety officer has released it.
