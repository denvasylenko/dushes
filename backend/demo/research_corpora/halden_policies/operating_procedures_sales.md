# Operating Procedures: Sales

The standard procedures of the Sales function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-SAL-01 Quote

Owner: Rowan Rendle, Sales. Last review: 13 May 2026. Next review after 24 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how quote is done in Sales, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 20 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 5 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 72 hours, until Quality decides. A problem that is not solved within 24 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The operator releases the label.
2. The team lead confirms the setting.
3. Quality enters in the system the count.
4. The specialist records the label within the same shift.
5. The head of department confirms the document against the checklist.
6. The shift leader measures the count with a second person.
7. The specialist enters in the system the document before the next step starts.
8. Quality signs off the label before the next step starts.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 3 years in the document store. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| backlog at month end | at most 2 per month | monthly |
| time to close | 95% | quarterly |
| first-time-right | at most 5 per month | weekly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one week only.
