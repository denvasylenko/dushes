# Site Annex: Garwyn

Local rules for the Garwyn site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

270 employees. Site director: Taylor Varnell. Safety officer: Riley Kirwan, +00 31 797 8904. Site emergency number: +00 11 597 6898. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 06:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 8 to 21 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

There is no canteen at this site; meal vouchers are issued as described in the Benefits Guide.
