# Site Annex: Norstead

Local rules for the Norstead site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

710 employees. Site director: Milan Osterby. Safety officer: Morgan Rendle, +00 31 432 7772. Site emergency number: +00 11 368 4388. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 07:00-15:30. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 4 to 22 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

## Eating

The canteen serves the daily menu at €3.90 for employees; the company pays the rest. No meal vouchers are issued at this site.

## Getting here

200 parking spaces, of which 4 have a charger. The shuttle from Norstead station leaves at 05:40, 13:40 and 21:35. Bicycles are kept in the shelter next to building A.
