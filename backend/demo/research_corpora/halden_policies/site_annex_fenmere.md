# Site Annex: Fenmere

Local rules for the Fenmere site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

960 employees. Site director: Jamie Rendle. Safety officer: Sasha Quarrie, +00 31 854 5832. Site emergency number: +00 11 376 8515. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 07:00-15:30. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 5 to 16 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

## Eating

The canteen serves the daily menu at €4.50 for employees; the company pays the rest. No meal vouchers are issued at this site.

## Getting here

130 parking spaces, of which 16 have a charger. The shuttle from Fenmere station leaves at 05:45, 13:35 and 21:35. Bicycles are kept in the shelter next to building B.
