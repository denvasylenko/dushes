# Operating Procedures: Procurement

The standard procedures of the Procurement function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-PRO-01 Purchase requisition

Owner: Riley Ironsby, Procurement. Last review: 25 March 2026. Next review after 24 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how purchase requisition is done in Procurement, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 90 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 13 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 24 hours, until the shift leader decides. A problem that is not solved within 8 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The head of department records the previous step against the checklist.
2. The team lead signs off the batch before the next step starts.
3. Quality photographs and files the setting.
4. The operator enters in the system the count.
5. The team lead records the label.
6. The shift leader photographs and files the setting before the next step starts.
7. The head of department measures the result before the next step starts.
8. The team lead enters in the system the document.
9. The safety officer records the count against the checklist.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 10 years in the maintenance system. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| backlog at month end | 98% | weekly |
| time to close | 92% | monthly |
| cost per case | 99% | quarterly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one batch only.

## SOP-PRO-02 Request for quotes

Owner: Sam Northcott, Procurement. Last review: 20 January 2026. Next review after 36 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how request for quotes is done in Procurement, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 20 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 3 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 48 hours, until Quality decides. A problem that is not solved within 4 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The specialist enters in the system the previous step.
2. The operator records the setting with a second person.
3. The shift leader measures the count with a second person.
4. The operator signs off the result against the checklist.
5. The shift leader measures the document.
6. The specialist confirms the batch with a second person.
7. The specialist measures the result within the same shift.
8. The head of department releases the result before the next step starts.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 2 years in the People portal. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| time to close | at most 3 per month | monthly |
| time to close | 85% | quarterly |
| cost per case | at most 10 per month | monthly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one week only.
