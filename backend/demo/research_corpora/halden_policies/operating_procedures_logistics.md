# Operating Procedures: Logistics

The standard procedures of the Logistics function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-LOG-01 Goods receipt

Owner: Toni Tregarth, Logistics. Last review: 7 March 2026. Next review after 12 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how goods receipt is done in Logistics, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 20 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 13 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 72 hours, until the shift leader decides. A problem that is not solved within 24 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The safety officer measures the batch against the checklist.
2. The safety officer measures the result with a second person.
3. The safety officer measures the previous step.
4. The safety officer checks the batch against the checklist.
5. The specialist releases the batch against the checklist.
6. The head of department confirms the previous step against the checklist.
7. The safety officer records the batch.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 5 years in the document store. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| on-time completion | 97% | quarterly |
| cost per case | at most 3 per month | quarterly |
| on-time completion | 85% | weekly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one shift only.
