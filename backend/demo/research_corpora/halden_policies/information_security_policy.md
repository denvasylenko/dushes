# Information Security Policy

Version 9, in force from 1 September 2025. Directive 2026-07 amends section 2.1 from 1 July 2026.

## 1 Classes of information

| Class | Examples | Sharing | Where it may be stored |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public | press releases, the website | none | any |
| Internal | the intranet, procedures | not outside the company without a reason | company devices and approved tools |
| Confidential | prices, contracts, personal data | encrypted when sent outside; need to know | company devices only |
| Secret | merger plans, unreleased designs | named recipients only; never by email | the secure data room only |

## 2 Access

### 2.1 Passwords

A password has at least 12 characters and is not reused on another service. Passwords are changed only when there is a sign of compromise. The password manager provided by IT is the only approved place to keep them.

### 2.2 Second factor

Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access, for email on a phone and for every administrator account.

### 2.3 Leaving the desk

Lock the screen when leaving the desk. Screens lock by themselves after 5 minutes.

## 3 Devices

Laptops are replaced after 4 years and phones after 3 years. Only software from the company store is installed. A lost or stolen device is reported to the service desk within 1 hour, at any time of day.

Private devices may be used for email and the calendar through the managed app, and for nothing else.

## 4 Incidents

Anything that may be a security incident (a suspicious email that was opened, data sent to the wrong person, an unknown device on the network) is reported to the service desk within 4 hours of noticing it. A personal data breach is assessed by the Data Protection Officer, who reports it to the authority within 72 hours where the law requires it.

## 5 Suppliers

A supplier that handles Confidential information signs a data processing agreement and answers the security questionnaire before the first data is shared. Access for suppliers ends with the contract and is reviewed every 6 months.

## 6 Working away from the site

Use the company VPN on any network that is not a Halden Works network. Do not work on Confidential documents where others can read the screen. Printing at home is not allowed for Confidential and Secret information.
