# Operating Procedures: Maintenance

The standard procedures of the Maintenance function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-MAI-01 Planned inspection

Owner: Jamie Osterby, Maintenance. Last review: 2 April 2026. Next review after 36 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how planned inspection is done in Maintenance, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 20 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 5 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 72 hours, until Quality decides. A problem that is not solved within 4 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The shift leader measures the document against the checklist.
2. The head of department checks the previous step with a second person.
3. The team lead confirms the count.
4. Quality signs off the previous step with a second person.
5. The team lead checks the previous step against the checklist.
6. The shift leader checks the setting before the next step starts.
7. The safety officer checks the document within the same shift.
8. The shift leader confirms the setting within the same shift.
9. The operator photographs and files the count.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 5 years in the ERP system. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| cost per case | 98% | quarterly |
| backlog at month end | 97% | weekly |
| time to close | 99% | quarterly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one batch only.
