# Operating Procedures: IT

The standard procedures of the IT function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-IT-01 New user account

Owner: Rowan Quarrie, IT. Last review: 6 March 2026. Next review after 24 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how new user account is done in IT, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 20 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 8 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 72 hours, until Quality decides. A problem that is not solved within 8 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The safety officer signs off the document before the next step starts.
2. The team lead signs off the setting.
3. The specialist checks the label.
4. The operator enters in the system the batch with a second person.
5. The safety officer measures the label.
6. The specialist confirms the label before the next step starts.
7. The operator releases the batch before the next step starts.
8. The specialist checks the label before the next step starts.
9. The specialist confirms the setting against the checklist.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 2 years in the quality system. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| repeat findings | 90% | weekly |
| first-time-right | 97% | quarterly |
| repeat findings | 92% | weekly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one batch only.
