# Job Profiles 2026

One profile for each job in the Job Grades and Salary Bands document: what the job is for, what the holder decides alone, and what training the role requires. Training codes refer to the Training Catalogue.

## Operator Assembly (G1)

**Purpose.** Delivers the work of Assembly at one workplace to the agreed quality, cost and dates.

**Decides alone.** Rework within the standard time; spending within the Approval Matrix limit for grade G1.

**Leads.** Nobody. Reports to the head of department.

**Needs.** A completed apprenticeship; no language requirement beyond the site's language. Required training: TC-018, TC-067.

**Time in grade.** Normally at least 18 months before a move to the next grade.

## Senior operator Assembly (G2)

**Purpose.** Delivers the work of Assembly at one workplace to the agreed quality, cost and dates.

**Decides alone.** Stopping a machine or a process for safety or quality; spending within the Approval Matrix limit for grade G2.
