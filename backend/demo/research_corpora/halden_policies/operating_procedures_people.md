# Operating Procedures: People

The standard procedures of the People function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-PEO-01 Job requisition

Owner: Sasha Kettridge, People. Last review: 14 May 2026. Next review after 24 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how job requisition is done in People, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 45 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 8 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 24 hours, until Quality decides. A problem that is not solved within 8 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The team lead measures the label.
2. The operator confirms the count within the same shift.
3. The safety officer measures the count.
4. The head of department signs off the batch with a second person.
5. The team lead enters in the system the previous step against the checklist.
6. The specialist signs off the previous step against the checklist.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 10 years in the maintenance system. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| repeat findings | at most 5 per month | quarterly |
| time to close | at most 10 per month | quarterly |
| first-time-right | at most 5 per month | quarterly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one week only.

## SOP-PEO-02 Offer letter

Owner: Jamie Quarrie, People. Last review: 17 April 2026. Next review after 36 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how offer letter is done in People, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 60 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 10 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 24 hours, until the shift leader decides. A problem that is not solved within 2 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. Quality confirms the setting.
2. The specialist enters in the system the previous step against the checklist.
3. The head of department enters in the system the document against the checklist.
4. The safety officer confirms the result.
5. The shift leader measures the document before the next step starts.
6. The safety officer enters in the system the batch before the next step starts.
7. The shift leader photographs and files the setting.
8. Quality releases the document within the same shift.
9. The safety officer signs off the batch before the next step starts.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 2 years in the People portal. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

|---|---|---|
| cost per case | at most 3 per month | quarterly |
| time to close | at most 2 per month | monthly |
| first-time-right | at most 10 per month | monthly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one shift only.
