# Operating Procedures: Finance

The standard procedures of the Finance function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-FIN-01 Supplier invoice booking

Owner: Avery Halloway, Finance. Last review: 3 January 2026. Next review after 24 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how supplier invoice booking is done in Finance, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 45 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 3 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 72 hours, until the shift leader decides. A problem that is not solved within 4 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. Quality confirms the batch against the checklist.
2. The team lead enters in the system the label before the next step starts.
3. Quality releases the setting.
4. The shift leader signs off the setting before the next step starts.
5. The specialist measures the label within the same shift.
6. The operator measures the previous step.
7. Quality checks the setting against the checklist.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 2 years in the document store. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| cost per case | 95% | monthly |
| backlog at month end | at most 2 per month | quarterly |
| on-time completion | at most 5 per month | quarterly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one week only.
