# Operating Procedures: Quality

The standard procedures of the Quality function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-QUA-01 Incoming inspection

Owner: Noel Sallow, Quality. Last review: 5 February 2026. Next review after 24 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how incoming inspection is done in Quality, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 30 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 10 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 72 hours, until the shift leader decides. A problem that is not solved within 24 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. Quality releases the document against the checklist.
2. The safety officer enters in the system the count against the checklist.
3. The specialist confirms the previous step.
4. The specialist photographs and files the setting.
5. The team lead photographs and files the batch against the checklist.
6. The head of department measures the setting.
7. The team lead records the count.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 3 years in the ERP system. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| on-time completion | at most 3 per month | quarterly |
| time to close | at most 10 per month | monthly |
| time to close | 85% | quarterly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one shift only.
