# Approval Matrix

Who may approve what, by grade. The limits are per order or per decision, excluding VAT. Above grade G8 the Management Board decides.

| Grade | Purchase orders up to | Leave and travel | Hiring | Write-offs up to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | none | - | - | none |
| G2 | none | - | - | none |
| G3 | €1,000 | - | - | €100 |
| G4 | €5,000 | own team | temporary staff up to 3 months | €500 |
| G5 | €15,000 | own team | temporary staff | €1,500 |
| G6 | €50,000 | own team | grades G1-G4 | €5,000 |
| G7 | €150,000 | own team | grades G1-G6 | €15,000 |
| G8 | €250,000 | own team | all grades | €25,000 |

## Stand-ins

An approver who is away for more than 3 working days names a stand-in of the same or a higher grade in the approval tool. A stand-in never approves their own request.

## Two signatures

Contracts that bind Halden Works for more than 12 months, and any order above €50,000, are signed by two people with signing authority, one of them from Finance or Legal.
