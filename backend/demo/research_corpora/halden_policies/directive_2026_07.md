# Directive 2026-07: amendments to three policies

Issued by the Management Board on 12 June 2026. In force from 1 July 2026. This directive prevails over the policies it amends until their next edition includes the changes.

## 1 Travel and Expenses Policy, section 3 (hotels)

The hotel cap rises to €165 per night including breakfast, and to €210 per night in Verdania City and outside Verdania. Stays that begin before 1 July 2026 keep the old cap for all their nights.

## 2 Procurement Policy, section 2 (quotes)

One quote is enough for orders below €4,000. Three written quotes are needed from €4,000 to below €25,000. The other thresholds do not change.

## 3 Information Security Policy, section 2.1 (passwords)

A password has at least 14 characters. Existing passwords that are shorter must be changed by 30 September 2026.

## 4 What does not change

The mileage rate, the per diem rates, the purchase order threshold and the approval limits stay as they are.
