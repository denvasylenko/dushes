Edition 2026, in force from 1 January 2026. It replaces the Staff Handbook 2025 for everything that happens on or after that date. Site annexes add local rules; a works agreement signed for one site prevails at that site over this handbook for the matters it covers.

## 2 Joining

### 2.1 Probation

The probation period is 4 months from the first working day. During probation either side may end the contract with 2 weeks' notice. A probation review is held with the line manager at the end of the second month and two weeks before the period ends.

### 2.2 Notice after probation

After probation the notice period is 2 months to the end of a calendar month, for both the employee and Halden Works. For grades G7 and G8 it is 4 months.

### 2.3 Documents

Before the first working day the People team needs proof of identity, the bank details for salary and the tax card. Salary is paid on the 27th of each month, or on the working day before it.

## 3 Leave

### 3.1 Annual leave

Full-time employees have 27 working days of paid annual leave in a calendar year. After 5 full years of service the entitlement rises by 2 days. Part-time staff get the same share as their share of full-time hours.

### 3.2 Carrying leave over

Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year and must be taken by 31 March. Days not taken by then lapse, unless the leave could not be taken because of illness or a refusal by the manager.

### 3.3 Booking leave

Leave is requested in the People portal. Requests of more than 10 consecutive working days need 6 weeks' notice. The manager answers within 5 working days; no answer in that time counts as approval.

### 3.4 Sickness

Tell your manager before the start of the shift. A medical certificate is needed from the third calendar day of an absence. Salary continues in full for the first 6 weeks of one illness; after that the health insurer pays sickness benefit.

### 3.5 Parental leave

Either parent may take up to 20 weeks of parental leave in the child's first year, of which 16 weeks are at full pay and the rest at 60% of salary. Tell the People team at least 8 weeks before the first day.

### 3.6 Special leave

| Event | Paid days |
|---|---|
| Own wedding or registered partnership | 3 |
| Birth of a child (the other parent) | 5 |
| Death of a partner, child or parent | 5 |
| Death of a sibling or grandparent | 2 |
| Moving home | 1 |
| Blood donation | the hours needed |

## 4 Working time

### 4.1 Hours

The full-time week is 38.5 hours. Office staff work flexible hours between 06:30 and 20:00 with core hours from 09:30 to 15:00. Production staff work the shift plan of their site (see the site annex).

### 4.2 Overtime

Overtime is work the manager has ordered or approved above the weekly hours. Grades G1 to G4 are paid a premium of 25% for overtime, or may take the time off at a rate of 1.25 hours for each hour. Grades G5 and above have overtime included in salary. Night work between 22:00 and 06:00 carries a premium of 20%.

## 4.3 Presence at the site

Office staff whose role allows remote work are expected at their site on at least 3 days in each calendar week. Line managers agree the days with the team.

## 5 Pay and review

Salaries are reviewed once a year, with effect from 1 April. The salary band for each grade is in the Job Grades and Salary Bands document. A bonus, where the scheme of the year provides one, is paid with the May salary.

## 6 Conduct

Treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with respect. Gifts from business partners may be accepted up to a value of €50 in a calendar year from one partner and must be entered in the gift register above €20. Cash and vouchers are never accepted.

A conflict of interest (a relative at a supplier, a second job with a competitor) is reported to the manager and to Compliance before any decision in which it could play a part.

## 7 Learning

Each employee has 5 paid training days and a training budget of €1,200 in a calendar year. Courses come from the Training Catalogue; a course outside it needs the approval of the People team.

## 8 Leaving

Return the laptop, the phone, the access badge and any keys on the last working day. Untaken leave is paid with the final salary; leave taken beyond the entitlement is deducted from it.

## 9 Speaking up

Concerns about safety, fraud or harassment can be raised with the manager, the People team or the confidential line +00 80 575 6784. Nobody is disadvantaged for a report made in good faith.
