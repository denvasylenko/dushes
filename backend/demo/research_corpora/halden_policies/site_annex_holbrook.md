# Site Annex: Holbrook

Local rules for the Holbrook site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

290 employees. Site director: Jordan Lowmoor. Safety officer: Avery Oakhurst, +00 31 381 8455. Site emergency number: +00 11 249 5792. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 06:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 5 to 18 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

## Eating

The canteen serves the daily menu at €5.20 for employees; the company pays the rest. No meal vouchers are issued at this site.

## Getting here

400 parking spaces, of which 16 have a charger. The shuttle from Holbrook station leaves at 05:35, 13:35 and 21:40. Bicycles are kept in the shelter next to building B.
