# Operating Procedures: Foundry

The standard procedures of the Foundry function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-FOU-01 Furnace charging

Owner: Casey Lowmoor, Foundry. Last review: 11 February 2026. Next review after 24 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how furnace charging is done in Foundry, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 20 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 5 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 72 hours, until the shift leader decides. A problem that is not solved within 8 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The operator confirms the previous step against the checklist.
2. The specialist measures the document before the next step starts.
3. The specialist confirms the count within the same shift.
4. The head of department photographs and files the batch within the same shift.
5. The operator enters in the system the document against the checklist.
6. The shift leader records the result against the checklist.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 5 years in the ERP system. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| time to close | 90% | monthly |
| backlog at month end | 95% | weekly |
| first-time-right | at most 2 per month | quarterly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one shift only.
