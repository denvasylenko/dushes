# Site Annex: Penmere

Local rules for the Penmere site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

530 employees. Site director: Kai Rendle. Safety officer: Noel Rendle, +00 31 143 6323. Site emergency number: +00 11 572 2758. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 06:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00, 22:00-06:00. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 5 to 16 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

## Eating

The canteen serves the daily menu at €5.20 for employees; the company pays the rest. No meal vouchers are issued at this site.

## Getting here

180 parking spaces, of which 9 have a charger. The shuttle from Penmere station leaves at 05:35, 13:35 and 21:35. Bicycles are kept in the shelter next to building B.
