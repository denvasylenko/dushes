# Site Annex: Kelmar

Local rules for the Kelmar site of Halden Works, 2026. The Staff Handbook and the group policies apply unless this annex says otherwise for a local matter.

## The site

700 employees. Site director: Sam Northcott. Safety officer: Jamie Northcott, +00 31 364 4605. Site emergency number: +00 11 480 5619. Reception is open from 06:00 to 20:00 on working days.

## Shifts

Production works these shifts: 06:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00, 22:00-06:00. Shift handover takes 10 minutes and is paid. The summer shutdown in 2026 is from 7 to 19 August; production staff take leave for it out of their yearly entitlement.

## Eating

The canteen serves the daily menu at €3.90 for employees; the company pays the rest. No meal vouchers are issued at this site.

## Getting here

320 parking spaces, of which 19 have a charger. The shuttle from Kelmar station leaves at 05:45, 13:35 and 21:40. Bicycles are kept in the shelter next to building C.
