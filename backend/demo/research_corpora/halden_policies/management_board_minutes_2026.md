# Management Board Minutes 2026 (extracts for staff)

Extracts of decisions that change a policy, published so that staff can see the reasons. The directive or the policy is the binding text.

## Meeting of 12 June 2026

### Hotel cap (Directive 2026-07, point 1)

Finance reported that in the twelve months to April 2026, 31% of hotel nights were booked above the cap of €140 and needed a site director's approval, against 9% two years earlier. The travel agency's survey of the 40 most used towns showed an average rate of €158 for a standard room with breakfast. The Board raised the cap to €165 so that approvals above the cap become the exception again, and decided against a cap by town because the travel tool cannot enforce one. The cap will be reviewed in June 2027.

### Single-quote threshold (Directive 2026-07, point 2)

Procurement reported 4,180 orders between €2,500 and €4,000 in 2025, worth 3% of total spend but taking 22% of buyers' time for collecting three quotes. An internal audit sample of 200 such orders found an average saving of 1.1% from the second and third quote. The Board raised the threshold to €4,000 and asked Procurement to audit a sample of single-quote orders every quarter. The rule against splitting orders stays.

### Password length (Directive 2026-07, point 3)

The security team reported that the cyber insurer requires passwords of at least 14 characters from the renewal on 1 October 2026, and that a test in March 2026 cracked 6% of the 12-character passwords in a copied test directory within a week. The Board set the minimum to 14 characters and kept the rule that passwords are not changed on a schedule.

### Mileage rate

The works council asked to raise the mileage rate from €0.42 per kilometre. The Board kept the rate because the tax-free limit in Verdania is €0.42, and asked People to correct the Benefits Guide, which still shows the 2024 rate.

## Meeting of 14 January 2026

### Annual leave and probation (Staff Handbook 2026)

The Board confirmed the changes agreed with the works council for the 2026 handbook: annual leave from 25 to 27 days, because two competitors in the region moved to 27 and 28 days and 14 of 60 leavers in 2025 named leave in their exit interview; probation from 6 to 4 months, in exchange for a longer notice period of 2 months after probation.

### Office days

The Board discussed presence at the site. People proposed two site days a week; Operations asked for three. No decision was taken; the item returns to the Board in September 2026. Until then managers apply the rule in force.
