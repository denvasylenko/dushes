# Remote Work Policy

Version 2, in force from 1 February 2026.

## 1 Who can work remotely

Employees whose tasks do not need them at a machine, a counter or a laboratory, after the probation period, and with the agreement of the line manager.

## 2 How much

Remote work is possible on up to 3 days in a calendar week; employees are at their site on at least 2 days in each week. Teams fix one common site day.

## 3 Where

Remote work is done from the home address registered with the People team. Work from another address in Verdania is allowed for up to 10 working days in a year. Work from abroad is allowed for up to 20 working days in a calendar year, only from countries on the People team's list, and must be approved by the People team at least 4 weeks ahead.

## 4 Equipment and costs

Halden Works provides a laptop, a headset and one screen. A flat allowance of €30 a month is paid to employees who work remotely on at least 8 days in the month. Furniture is not paid for.

## 5 Being reachable

Remote staff are reachable during core hours and attend site meetings when the manager asks with 2 working days' notice.

**Remote work condition 1.** We may rely on information you give us. You must tell us promptly when that information changes, because a change may affect the service or the price.

**Remote work condition 2.** Staff are trained to explain these terms in plain language. If anything is unclear, ask for an explanation before you rely on your understanding of it.

**Remote work condition 3.** Nothing in this document limits any right that cannot be limited under the law that applies to it. If a provision is found invalid, the rest remains in force.

**Remote work condition 4.** Copies of earlier versions of this document are kept in the archive and can be requested. They apply only to the period stated on their cover.

**Remote work condition 5.** The parties will try to resolve any dispute by discussion between senior representatives before starting formal proceedings.

**Remote work condition 6.** A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of that right, and a single or partial exercise does not prevent any further exercise of it.

**Remote work condition 7.** If there is a conflict between a schedule and the main text, the schedule applies for the matter it covers and the main text applies for everything else.

**Remote work condition 8.** We process personal data as described in our privacy notice. We keep records only for as long as we need them for the purposes stated there.
