# Travel and Expenses Policy

Version 6, in force from 1 January 2026. Directive 2026-07 amends section 3 from 1 July 2026; where the two differ, the directive applies.

For anything about travel and expenses this policy prevails over other Halden Works guides, including the Benefits Guide.

## 1 Before you travel

Every trip is requested in the travel tool and approved by the line manager before booking. Trips outside Verdania also need the approval of the site director. Book through the travel tool at least 14 days ahead; a later booking needs a reason.

## 2 Getting there

Rail is the standard for journeys of up to 4 hours by train. Flights are booked in economy. Business class is allowed only for a flight segment of more than 6 hours and only for grade G7 and above, or on medical grounds confirmed by the company doctor.

Use of a private car is paid at €0.42 per kilometre, up to 400 km per journey; above that a hire car or rail must be used. Taxis are for journeys where public transport would take more than 30 minutes longer or carries a safety risk.

## 3 Hotels

The hotel cap is €140 per night including breakfast, and €190 per night in Verdania City and outside Verdania. A night above the cap needs the approval of the site director before the stay.

## 4 Meals and per diem

A per diem covers meals and small costs for each full day away; half is paid for the day of departure and the day of return. The rate depends on the destination and the grade band and is in the Per Diem Table. A meal provided by the host or included in a ticket reduces the per diem: breakfast by 20%, lunch by 40%, dinner by 40%.

## 5 Claiming

Claims go into the expense tool with a receipt for every item above €10. A claim must be filed within 30 days of the end of the trip; later claims are paid only with the approval of the Finance director. A cash advance of up to €1,500 can be requested for trips of 5 days or more.

## 6 What is not paid

Fines, minibar, pay TV, personal phone calls, travel insurance bought privately, and costs for an accompanying person.

## 7 Entertaining guests

Meals with business guests are paid up to €65 per person. The claim names every guest and the business reason. Alcohol is limited to what goes with the meal.
