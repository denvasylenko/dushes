# Operating Procedures: Engineering

The standard procedures of the Engineering function at every Halden Works site, edition 2026. A site may add steps in its annex but may not drop any. Each procedure has a code, an owner and a review date.

## SOP-ENG-01 Design change request

Owner: Casey Larkmoor, Engineering. Last review: 28 May 2026. Next review after 12 months.

### Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how design change request is done in Engineering, who does what and what is recorded. It applies to all shifts.

### Limits

The whole procedure takes at most 30 minutes on a normal day. Where a check takes a sample, the sample is 10 units of each batch. A result outside the limits puts the batch on hold for up to 24 hours, until the shift leader decides. A problem that is not solved within 8 hours is escalated to the head of department.

### Steps

1. The safety officer records the setting before the next step starts.
2. The shift leader enters in the system the setting against the checklist.
3. The team lead photographs and files the batch.
4. The safety officer measures the document before the next step starts.
5. The shift leader checks the batch with a second person.
6. The head of department confirms the document before the next step starts.
7. The shift leader enters in the system the document against the checklist.
8. The shift leader confirms the document with a second person.

### Records

The record of each run is kept for 3 years in the quality system. A run without a record counts as not done.

### Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported |
|---|---|---|
| on-time completion | 95% | monthly |
| repeat findings | at most 2 per month | weekly |
| on-time completion | 85% | quarterly |

### Deviations

A deviation from this procedure needs the consent of the owner beforehand, in writing, and is valid for one shift only.
