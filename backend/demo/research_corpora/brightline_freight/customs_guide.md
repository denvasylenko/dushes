# Customs Guide for Exports to Neighbouring Countries

March 2026. Applies to Zone 6 consignments.

## Documents

Every Zone 6 consignment needs a commercial invoice in two copies with the value, the currency, the commodity code and the country of origin of each item. Consignments above €1,000 also need an export declaration.

Duties and import taxes in the destination country are paid by the receiver unless the booking says Delivered Duty Paid (DDP). For DDP shipments Brightline charges a disbursement fee of 2.5% of the duties, minimum €10.

## Prohibited and restricted goods

Weapons, counterfeit goods, protected species and goods subject to sanctions are prohibited. Food, plants, medicines and alcohol are restricted and need extra certificates.

## Delays

Customs checks can add 1 to 3 business days to the transit time. Such delays do not count against the service levels.
