# Dangerous Goods Instructions for Customers

Version 2.3, March 2026. These instructions are part of the Master Services Agreement.

## Classes we accept

| ADR class | Description | Accepted |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Explosives | No |
| 2 | Gases (aerosols, gas cylinders) | Yes, with declaration |
| 3 | Flammable liquids | Yes, with declaration |
| 4.1 | Flammable solids | Yes, with declaration |
| 5.1 | Oxidising substances | By prior approval only |
| 6.1 | Toxic substances | Yes, with declaration |
| 6.2 | Infectious substances | No |
| 7 | Radioactive material | No |
| 8 | Corrosive substances | Yes, with declaration |
| 9 | Miscellaneous, including lithium batteries | Yes, see below |

## Declaration

Send the dangerous goods declaration through the customer portal at least 24 hours before collection. Without a declaration we refuse the consignment at collection and charge a failed collection fee of €35.

## Lithium batteries

Batteries packed with or inside equipment (UN3481, UN3091) are accepted with a declaration. Stand-alone lithium-ion batteries (UN3480) and lithium metal batteries (UN3090) are accepted only with prior approval from the dangerous goods desk, up to 30 kg gross per package. Damaged or defective batteries are never accepted.

## Limited quantities

Goods in limited quantities are accepted up to 8 tonnes gross per vehicle. Packages must show the limited quantity mark.

## Surcharge

A dangerous goods surcharge of €28 per consignment applies, or €12 for limited quantities.

## Emergency

In an emergency during carriage, our drivers follow the written instructions carried in the cab. The dangerous goods desk can be reached on +00 31 431 9330 (24 hours).
