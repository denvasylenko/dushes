# Network Calendar 2026

Public holidays in Verdania and how the network works on them.

| Date | Holiday | Depots |
|---|---|---|
| 1 January | New Year's Day | closed |
| 14 March | Founders' Day | closed |
| Friday before Easter | Spring holiday | closed |
| 1 May | Labour Day | closed |
| 21 June | Midsummer | open, no collections |
| 5 September | Verdania Day | closed |
| 24 December | Christmas Eve | open until 12:00 |
| 25 December | Christmas Day | closed |
| 26 December | Second Christmas Day | closed |
| 31 December | New Year's Eve | open until 14:00 |

Consignments collected the business day before a closed day are delivered on the next business day after it. There are no Saturday deliveries on the weekend of a closed Friday or Monday.
