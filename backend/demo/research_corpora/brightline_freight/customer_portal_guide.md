# Customer Portal User Guide

For the Brightline customer portal, release 2026.2.

## Book a collection

2. Confirm the address.
3. Choose the details.
4. Review the consignment.
5. Review the consignment.
6. Enter the date.

Changes are saved at once and shown in the activity log.

## Changes to a delivery address

A delivery address can be changed free of charge until the consignment is loaded for delivery. After that, a re-delivery fee of €14 applies.
