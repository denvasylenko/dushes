## Drill driver DD-0713

Article DD-0713. Made for occasional use around the house. In the box: a hex key, no battery or charger, a dust bag.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Max torque | 85 Nm |
| No-load speed | 2500 rpm |
| Chuck | 10 mm |
| Weight without battery | 2.0 kg |
| Platform | 230 V mains |
| Motor | brushed |
| Protection | IPX4 |
| Sound pressure | 95 dB(A) |
| Class | professional |
| Warranty | 3 years when registered |

### Use and care

Designed for 15 minutes of work followed by 5 minutes of rest. The vibration value is 8.7 m/s2 (uncertainty 1.5 m/s2); at this value the daily exposure limit is reached after about 3 hours of trigger time. Use and store between -20 and 50 degrees. The cable is 2.5 m long; use an extension lead of at least 1.5 mm2 and at most 50 m. Have the tool inspected by a service partner every 200 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first; the inspection covers the bearings and the switch. Clean the air slots with dry air at no more than 4 bar. Check the guard and the fixings before each use.

Fits: AC-133, AC-357, AC-579, AC-686. Let the tool reach full speed before it touches the work. Unchanged since 2025.
