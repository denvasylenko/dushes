## Line laser MS-0330

Article MS-0330. Made for serial work on a line. In the box: a dust bag, a side handle, a charger.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Range | 30 m |
| Accuracy | 0.3 mm/m |
| Runtime | 18 h |
| Weight | 0.6 kg |
| Platform | V12 battery |
| Motor | brushed |
| Protection | IP54 |
| Sound pressure | not applicable |
| Class | professional |
| Warranty | 2 years when registered |

### Use and care

Designed for short bursts of up to 5 minutes. The vibration value is 7.9 m/s2 (uncertainty 1.5 m/s2); at this value the daily exposure limit is reached after about 3 hours of trigger time. Use and store between -20 and 50 degrees. Orrin recommends a 2.0 Ah pack of the V12 platform for this tool; smaller packs fit but shorten the runtime and may switch off under full load. Have the tool inspected by a service partner every 200 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first; the inspection covers the chuck, bearings and the cooling paths. Clean the air slots with dry air at no more than 3 bar. Never immerse the tool in water.

Fits: AC-263, AC-403, AC-898. Not for use in explosive atmospheres. Unchanged since 2025.
