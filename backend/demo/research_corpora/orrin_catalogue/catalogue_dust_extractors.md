## Dust extractor DX-0713

Article DX-0713. Made for metalwork. In the box: a side handle, a dust bag, a depth stop.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Tank | 13 l |
| Airflow | 48 l/s |
| Suction | 180 mbar |
| Weight | 6.0 kg |
| Platform | 230 V mains |
| Motor | brushless |
| Protection | IP54 |
| Sound pressure | 87 dB(A) |
| Class | professional |
| Warranty | 3 years when registered |

### Use and care

Designed for 30 minutes of work followed by 10 minutes of rest. The vibration value is 2.2 m/s2 (uncertainty 1.5 m/s2); at this value the daily exposure limit is reached after about 41 hours of trigger time. Use and store between -10 and 40 degrees. The cable is 5 m long; use an extension lead of at least 1.5 mm2 and at most 50 m. Have the tool inspected by a service partner every 200 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first; the inspection covers the bearings and the switch. Clean the air slots with dry air at no more than 3 bar. Never immerse the tool in water.

Fits: AC-153, AC-773, AC-853. Let the tool reach full speed before it touches the work. New in 2026.
