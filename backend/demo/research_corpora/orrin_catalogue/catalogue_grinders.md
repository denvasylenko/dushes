| Sound pressure | 94 dB(A) |
| Class | professional |
| Warranty | 3 years when registered |

### Use and care

Designed for continuous work. The vibration value is 5.6 m/s2 (uncertainty 1.5 m/s2); at this value the daily exposure limit is reached after about 6 hours of trigger time. Use and store between -5 and 40 degrees. The cable is 7.5 m long; use an extension lead of at least 1.5 mm2 and at most 50 m. Have the tool inspected by a service partner every 150 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first; the inspection covers the chuck, bearings and the cooling paths. Clean the air slots with dry air at no more than 2 bar. Check the guard and the fixings before each use.

Fits: AC-523, AC-683. Use only with the guard fitted. Updated electronics in 2026.
