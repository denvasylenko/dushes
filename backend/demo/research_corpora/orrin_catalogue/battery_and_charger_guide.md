# Battery and Charger Guide 2026

## The three platforms

V12 is for compact tools, V18 is the main professional platform, V36 is for heavy tools and the garden range. A pack fits only tools of its own platform, with one exception: two V18 packs of the same capacity can power a V36 tool through the adapter AD-1236. V12 packs fit nothing but V12 tools. V36 packs cannot be used on V18 tools.

## Chargers

CH-1218 charges V12 and V18 packs at 4 A. CH-1218F charges them at 8 A and cools the pack while charging; it must not be used for the 2.0 Ah packs BP-1220 and BP-1820. CH-3600 charges V36 packs only.

## Life and care

A pack keeps at least 80% of its capacity for 1000 charging cycles when it is stored between 5 and 25 degrees and not left empty. Charge only between 0 and 40 degrees. Store packs at about half charge when they are not used for more than a month.

## Transport

Packs above 100 Wh (BP-1880, BP-3640, BP-3680) travel as dangerous goods when sent by air; by road no special paper is needed for up to 4 packs in one parcel.
