## Chainsaw GD-0733

Article GD-0733. Made for site work in all weathers. In the box: a depth stop, a carrying case, a bit set.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Cutting length or width | 490 mm |
| Speed | 53 m/s |
| Sound power | 87 dB(A) |
| Weight without battery | 15.7 kg |
| Platform | 230 V mains |
| Motor | brushless |
| Protection | IP20 |
| Sound pressure | 94 dB(A) |
| Class | professional |
| Warranty | 3 years when registered |

### Use and care

Designed for short bursts of up to 5 minutes. The vibration value is 2.0 m/s2 (uncertainty 1.5 m/s2); at this value the daily exposure limit is reached after about 50 hours of trigger time. Use and store between -10 and 40 degrees. The cable is 7.5 m long; use an extension lead of at least 1.5 mm2 and at most 30 m. Have the tool inspected by a service partner every 250 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first; the inspection covers the electronics and the seals. Clean the air slots with dry air at no more than 4 bar. Keep the handles free of oil and grease.

Fits: AC-267, AC-517, AC-543, AC-702. Use only with the guard fitted. Updated electronics in 2026.
