## Demolition hammer RH-0711

Article RH-0711. Made for serial work on a line. In the box: a dust bag, a guide rail adapter, a blade.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Impact energy | 7.8 J |
| Max drilling in concrete | 52 mm |
| Impact rate | 3000 bpm |
| Weight | 3.7 kg |
| Platform | 230 V mains |
| Motor | brushed |
| Protection | IPX4 |
| Sound pressure | 69 dB(A) |
| Class | home and garden |
| Warranty | 2 years |

### Use and care

Designed for short bursts of up to 5 minutes. The vibration value is 12.0 m/s2 (uncertainty 1.5 m/s2); at this value the daily exposure limit is reached after about 1 hours of trigger time. Use and store between -20 and 50 degrees. The cable is 4 m long; use an extension lead of at least 1.5 mm2 and at most 50 m. Have the tool inspected by a service partner every 300 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first; the inspection covers the brushes and the gearbox grease. Clean the air slots with dry air at no more than 3 bar. Keep the handles free of oil and grease.

Fits: AC-558, AC-647. Wear eye and hearing protection. Unchanged since 2025.
