## Drywall sander SA-0787

Article SA-0787. Made for serial work on a line. In the box: a hex key, a depth stop, a carrying case.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Pad size | 194 mm |
| Orbit | 5.0 mm |
| Oscillations | 16000 opm |
| Weight | 4.0 kg |
| Platform | 230 V mains |
| Motor | brushed |
| Protection | IP20 |
| Sound pressure | 86 dB(A) |
| Class | professional |
| Warranty | 3 years when registered |

### Use and care

Designed for short bursts of up to 5 minutes. The vibration value is 3.4 m/s2 (uncertainty 1.5 m/s2); at this value the daily exposure limit is reached after about 17 hours of trigger time. Use and store between 0 and 40 degrees. The cable is 7.5 m long; use an extension lead of at least 1.5 mm2 and at most 50 m. Have the tool inspected by a service partner every 400 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first; the inspection covers the bearings and the switch. Clean the air slots with dry air at no more than 4 bar. Check the guard and the fixings before each use.

Fits: AC-296, AC-567. Let the tool reach full speed before it touches the work. Unchanged since 2025.
