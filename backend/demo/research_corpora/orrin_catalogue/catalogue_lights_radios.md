## Site radio LR-1132

Article LR-1132. Made for roofing and timber frame. In the box: two batteries, a guide rail adapter, a dust bag.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Luminous flux | 1050 lm |
| Runtime on a 5.0 Ah pack | 27 h |
| Protection | 62 IP |
| Weight | 3.2 kg |
| Platform | 230 V mains |
| Motor | brushed |
| Protection | IP56 |
| Sound pressure | 78 dB(A) |
| Class | professional |
| Warranty | 3 years when registered |

### Use and care

Designed for short bursts of up to 5 minutes. The vibration value is 11.8 m/s2 (uncertainty 1.5 m/s2); at this value the daily exposure limit is reached after about 1 hours of trigger time. Use and store between 0 and 40 degrees. The cable is 7.5 m long; use an extension lead of at least 1.5 mm2 and at most 30 m. Have the tool inspected by a service partner every 150 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first; the inspection covers the chuck, bearings and the cooling paths. Clean the air slots with dry air at no more than 2 bar. Keep the handles free of oil and grease.

Fits: AC-624, AC-704. Use only with the guard fitted. Updated electronics in 2026.
