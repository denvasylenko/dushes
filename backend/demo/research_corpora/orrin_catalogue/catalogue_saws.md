| Motor | brushless |
| Protection | IP56 |
| Sound pressure | 96 dB(A) |
| Class | home and garden |
| Warranty | 2 years |

### Use and care

Designed for short bursts of up to 5 minutes. The vibration value is 3.2 m/s2 (uncertainty 1.5 m/s2); at this value the daily exposure limit is reached after about 20 hours of trigger time. Use and store between -10 and 40 degrees. The cable is 4 m long; use an extension lead of at least 1.5 mm2 and at most 30 m. Have the tool inspected by a service partner every 300 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first; the inspection covers the electronics and the seals. Clean the air slots with dry air at no more than 3 bar. Never immerse the tool in water.

Fits: AC-558, AC-738, AC-840. Not for use in explosive atmospheres. Updated electronics in 2026.
