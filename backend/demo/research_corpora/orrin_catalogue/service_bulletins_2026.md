# Service Bulletins 2026

Bulletins correct or add to the catalogue and the manuals. Where a bulletin and the catalogue differ, the bulletin applies.

## SB-2026-01: recall of battery packs BP-1840

Issued 20 January 2026. Packs BP-1840 with serial numbers from B24100000 to B24359999 can overheat while charging on the fast charger CH-1218F. Stop using these packs. Orrin replaces them free of charge, also after the warranty has ended; order the replacement on the website or at any dealer. Packs outside this serial range are not affected.

## SB-2026-02: chuck key for bench drills

Issued 3 February 2026. The chuck key shipped with bench drills from January 2026 is 1 mm longer. No action is needed.

## SB-2026-03: corrections to the Catalogue 2026

Issued 2 March 2026.

- DD-1860: the maximum torque is 130 Nm, not 140 Nm as printed.
- RH-1826: the weight is 3.4 kg, not 3.1 kg as printed.
