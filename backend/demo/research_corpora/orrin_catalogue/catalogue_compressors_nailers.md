| Platform | 230 V mains |
| Motor | brushless |
| Protection | IP20 |
| Sound pressure | 88 dB(A) |
| Class | home and garden |
| Warranty | 2 years |

### Use and care

Designed for 15 minutes of work followed by 5 minutes of rest. The vibration value is 10.7 m/s2 (uncertainty 1.5 m/s2); at this value the daily exposure limit is reached after about 2 hours of trigger time. Use and store between 0 and 40 degrees. The cable is 4 m long; use an extension lead of at least 1.5 mm2 and at most 25 m. Have the tool inspected by a service partner every 400 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first; the inspection covers the brushes and the gearbox grease. Clean the air slots with dry air at no more than 3 bar. Never immerse the tool in water.

Fits: AC-507, AC-548, AC-614. Use only with the guard fitted. Updated electronics in 2026.
