# Veltra HX Maintenance Manual

Edition 3, January 2026. This edition replaces Field Service Note FSN-2023-11 on oil checks and all earlier maintenance plans.

## 1 The plan

| Task | Interval | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Clean the air-side heat exchanger and check the fins | every 3 months in dusty places, otherwise yearly | operator or technician |
| Clean the strainer | 4 weeks after commissioning, then yearly | technician |
| Leak test of the refrigerant circuit | every 12 months; every 6 months for units with a charge above 5.0 kg | certified technician |
| Compressor oil level and acidity | every 12 months | certified technician |
| Electrical terminals: tighten and thermal scan | yearly | technician |
| Safety chain test (high pressure switch, flow switch, leak detector) | yearly | certified technician |
| Hot water tank anode | every 24 months | technician |
| Water quality: pH, hardness, glycol share | yearly | technician |
| Fan bearings | replace at 30,000 operating hours | technician |
| Compressor overhaul or replacement | assess at 60,000 operating hours | Veltra service |
| Condensate drain and heater | before each winter | operator or technician |
| Firmware | check for updates yearly | technician |

Which models fall under the shorter leak test interval follows from the refrigerant charge in the installation manual, section 2.

## 2 Why the intervals matter

The leak test is a legal duty for the operator; a missed test can void the insurance of the building. The oil check was moved from 24 to 12 months in 2026 after field returns showed acid formation in units that ran mostly at part load. The warranty on the compressor (5 years) depends on a documented oil check at each interval.

## 3 The maintenance log

Every task is entered in the log in the controller (service menu 4) and in the paper log kept with the unit. Keep the log for the life of the unit.

## 4 Taking the unit out of service

For a shutdown of more than 4 weeks in winter, either drain the outdoor water circuit or keep the frost protection running (the controller supply must stay on). Recover the refrigerant before scrapping; only certified staff may do so.
