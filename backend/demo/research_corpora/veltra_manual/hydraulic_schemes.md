# Veltra HX Hydraulic Schemes

The twelve schemes that P020 selects. Each lists what the controller expects to find; a missing sensor gives a fault at start-up.

## Scheme 1: one heating circuit, no buffer (not allowed for HX-50 and HX-80)

Sensors: boiler, buffer bottom, hot water tank, mixed circuit 1, mixed circuit 2, outdoor. Outputs: boiler release, buffer charge pump, diverter valve. Minimum buffer volume: as in the installation manual, plus 20% where a pool is connected. Typical P-settings: P452 = 4, P455 = 49.
