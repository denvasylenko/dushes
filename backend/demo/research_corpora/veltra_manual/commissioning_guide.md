# Veltra HX Commissioning Guide

Firmware 2.4. For the certified technician. Work through the steps in order; do not start the compressor before step 13.

## Steps

1. Check the transport locks on the compressor are removed (three red brackets).
2. Check clearances and the safety zone against the installation manual.
3. Check the strainer is fitted in the return and its valves are open.
4. Check the system pressure cold: 1.5 bar.
5. Bleed the circuit at the highest point and at the unit's bleed valve.
6. Check the supply: phase sequence, voltage within 5% on all phases.
7. Switch on the controller supply and wait for the self-test (about 90 seconds).
8. Set the language, date and time (P001 to P004).
9. Select the hydraulic scheme (P020) and the heat pump model (P021).
10. Run the pump test (service menu 3.1) and read the flow: it must be at least the minimum flow of the model.
11. Set the heating curve (P100 to P104).
12. Set the domestic hot water target (P110) and the anti-legionella program (P112, P113).
13. Start the compressor in test mode (service menu 3.4) for 15 minutes.
14. Read suction and discharge pressure, superheat and subcooling; compare with the table below.
15. Trigger a manual defrost (service menu 3.6) when the outdoor temperature is below 7 degrees.
16. Check the condensate drains freely.
17. Set the utility block and the quiet hours if asked for (P300 to P306).
18. Save the parameter set to the USB stick and leave a copy with the operator.
19. Fill in the commissioning report and send it to Veltra.

## Reference values at A7/W35, full speed

| Model | Suction pressure | Discharge pressure | Superheat | Subcooling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HX-20 | 4.3 to 5.4 bar | 15.5 to 19.2 bar | 4 to 8 K | 3 to 6 K |
| HX-35 | 4.3 to 5.6 bar | 16.0 to 20.0 bar | 4 to 8 K | 3 to 6 K |
| HX-50 | 4.1 to 5.4 bar | 16.0 to 19.2 bar | 4 to 8 K | 3 to 6 K |
| HX-80 | 4.5 to 5.9 bar | 16.0 to 20.0 bar | 4 to 8 K | 3 to 6 K |

## Common mistakes

A flow below the minimum is the most frequent cause of high-pressure faults in the first week: check the strainer after 4 weeks. A wrong hydraulic scheme in P020 makes the diverter valve work the wrong way round. A buffer tank that is too small shows as fault F212 on the first frosty morning.

## The report

The report names the installer, the serial number, the firmware, the measured flow, the pressures above and the parameter set. It reaches Veltra within 14 days of commissioning.
